# Circular economy problems and opportunities in large-appliance aftersales (India first, then global; as of Nov 2026)

Method note: ~17 tool calls (web searches plus page fetches). Several claims come from search-result summaries rather than fully read pages and are tagged "(snippet-level)". Anything without a URL is in Gaps. "Inference" sections are mine, not sourced.

## 1. Why large-appliance refurbishment is hard, and which business models work (Underdog, Back Market, OEM certified, India players)

### Takeaway
Large-appliance refurb is a logistics-and-trust problem more than a technology one. Players that work (Underdog in France, Whirlpool certified refurbished, Rentomojo's rental flywheel in India) control sourcing, testing, and the warranty themselves. Hard unit economics for Underdog or any Indian appliance refurbisher are NOT publicly available.

### Cited Findings
- Underdog (France, Nantes, founded Aug 2022) sources, repairs and refurbishes pre-owned appliances and sells via its own site plus a Nantes store, managing sourcing through logistics to sale. Funding is reported inconsistently (EUR 7.0m seed per Dealroom vs $7.6M on another directory). — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/underdog_), [Combak](https://www.combak.co/marchands/underdog), [Daphni](https://www.daphni.com/?p=10235) (Daphni page lists Underdog as portfolio only; no detail)
- Underdog's own materials claim its investment in Back Market gave it grounding in refurbished and that white goods are "very underserved" by refurbishment (snippet-level). — [Daphni](https://www.daphni.com/?p=10235)
- Underdog product page: 2-year warranty (parts, labour, repairs) included, 5-year extension for EUR 99. — [Underdog product page](https://underdog.shop/products/seche-linge-pompe-a-chaleur-9-kg-reconditionne-whirlpool-w7d94wbfr-bd657)
- Back Market sells large appliances (fridges from Samsung, Whirlpool, LG, Siemens, Beko; dishwashers) through third-party refurbishers with a 1-year minimum seller warranty, 12 or 24 months on some. I found no dated "category launch" announcement. — [Back Market IE large appliances](https://backmarket.ie/en-ie/l/large-appliances/eca9f921-519c-4f2d-b230-93fecbaf86e3), [Back Market US refrigerators](https://www.backmarket.com/en-us/l/refrigerators/9700eccc-cb96-4207-a4cc-fc5442d8f7f6)
- OEM certified refurb: Whirlpool US offers 1-year limited parts-and-labour warranty and 15-day free returns on certified refurbished units. — [Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/refurbished-appliances.html)
- Generic liquidator benchmarks (vendor marketing, weak): overstock fridges wholesale ~$120-450/unit, platform fee 8-12%, 20-25 min test per unit at $30-40. — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/overstock-refrigerators)
- Costify (New Delhi, founded 2022): refurbished fridges, washing machines, ACs, microwaves; 8,000+ customers; priced 50-70% below new; over Rs 4 crore annual revenue; angel round; ITI-graduate technicians trained in 8-12 weeks with AI-assisted SOPs; margins and warranty length not disclosed; plans 1-year warranty within two years; in talks with OEMs on returns/seconds/warranty stock; says OEM partnerships are rare and the segment suffers mistrust and jugaad repair. — [Startuppedia](https://startuppedia.in/startup-stories/sibling-duo-from-delhi-that-started-a-refurbishment-startup-for-home-appliances-raise-funding-now-clocking-rs-4-cr-annual-revenue-10894704)
- Older India attempts: Refabd (used products at 50-70% off MRP, 2017), Zefo (refurbished furniture/appliances, Rs 40 cr Series A led by Sequoia India, 2016; later reported $9M more), Guarented (rent and sell, started with fridge, microwave, TV, washing machine). Outcomes/survival of these not verified. — [YourStory Refabd](https://yourstory.com/2017/09/refabd-secondhand-products-discounts), [e27 Zefo](https://e27.co/zefo-raises-us6m-to-source-you-refurbished-furniture-and-home-appliances-20161115/), [Startuppedia/Guarented summary via search](https://startuppedia.in/startup-stories/brother-sister-duo-from-delhi-start-a-spinny-like-startup-for-refurbished-fridges-washing-machines-acs-serve-5000-customers-in-1-year-9774099)
- ReFit Global (Delhi): mainly phones but also large appliances; reported Rs 200 cr revenue FY23 (snippet-level, unverified); raised $1M from a UAE family office. — [Siliconindia](https://www.siliconindia.com/news/startups/an-delhibased-startup-refit-global-is-nailing-the-secondhand-device-market-nid-222925-cid-19.html), [Newskart](https://www.newskart.com/refit-global-raises-1-million-while-curvetai-secures-rs-6-crore-to-build-scalable-tech-platforms/)
- Qarmatek raised an undisclosed $1M to expand electronics renewal into consumer appliances. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=466682)

### Inferences
- The stated difficulty drivers you listed (logistics of bulky goods, grading, warranty risk, labour vs new price) are consistent with the above, but I found no source that quantifies them for appliances. Costify's 50-70% discount to new implies thin room for labour and logistics if sourcing is at scrap-plus prices (inference).
- Warranty length is the competitive signal: Underdog 2 years, Whirlpool and Back Market 1 year, Costify not yet 1 year. Warranty cost is the largest unknown liability.
- OEM take-back/returns/seconds stock is the cheapest feedstock and OEMs rarely partner in India (Costify's own claim), which is an opportunity.

### Gaps
- No public per-unit cost, gross margin, return rate or warranty-claim rate for Underdog, Back Market appliances, or any Indian refurbisher. Underdog press interviews not found.
- Back Market large-appliance launch date and volumes not found.
- Zefo/Refabd/Guarented current status unknown.

## 2. Trade-in/buyback funnel, informal leakage, kabadiwala pricing, formalisation

### Takeaway
Old appliances are worth real money to scrap dealers (metal body plus a priced compressor) and exchange discounts are modest, so leakage to kabadiwalas is rational. Formal channels (OEM exchange via Attero/Selsmart) exist but pay in discount, not cash.

### Cited Findings
- Over 95% of India's e-waste is said to be handled informally (Wikipedia/Eco-Business). Attero's CEO says informal share fell from ~99% to ~75% in four years (Nov 2024). Toxics Link estimates >90% of collection and initial dismantling is informal. Sources conflict on the level. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_waste_in_India), [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their), [Context/TRF](https://www.context.news/just-transition/in-delhis-e-waste-hub-indias-informal-workers-lose-business)
- Informal operators pay cash, carry no GST and no compliance cost, so outbid formal recyclers at source (18% GST applies only to registered businesses). — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- Kabadiwala/scrap-guide rates (blogs, not verified market data): fridge metal body Rs 18-40/kg plus compressor Rs 300-700; window AC Rs 2,500-5,000 and single-door fridge Rs 1,500-2,500 claimed; one IndiaMART listing shows AC scrap Rs 100/kg, inconsistent. — [DigiRake Delhi](https://knowledge.digirake.com/refrigerator/sell-old-refrigerator-delhi), [DigiRake Mysuru](https://knowledge.digirake.com/refrigerator/sell-old-refrigerator-mysuru), [ScrapRates](https://scraprates.in/blog/sell-old-ac-fridge-washing-machine-scrap), [IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/devnarayanpastibhandar/electronic-scrap.html)
- Store exchange offers typically Rs 2,000-5,000 off a new fridge (blog claim). — [DigiRake](https://knowledge.digirake.com/refrigerator/sell-old-refrigerator-delhi)
- Brand exchange programmes: Lloyd Eco Exchange with Selsmart (Attero); LG exchange valuation and pickup handled by Attero. — [Fonearena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/?p=473680), [pv-magazine India](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/press-releases/selsmart-by-attero-and-havells-lloyd-launch-lloyd-eco-exchange-program-to-enable-responsible-appliance-upgrades-across-india), [LG T&C](https://www.lg.com/in/exchangeoffers/tnc)
- Government AC swap scheme for 10+ year-old units was reported in drafting (July 2025); launch not confirmed in what I found. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/govt-ac-swap-scheme-energy-efficient-cooling-power-ministry-bee-125071100607_1.html)
- UNDP/GEF project with MeitY plans to test replacement-rebate business models and gender-responsive upgrading of informal sector. — [UNDP India](https://www.undp.org/india/press-releases/india-advances-transition-circular-economy-electronics-sector-gef-and-undp-support)
- Attero proposes a GST Reverse Charge Mechanism to create a paper trail on scrap purchases (levels the field against informal). — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- Scale: ~1.75 million t e-waste in FY2023-24 (CPCB via DTE), recycling share 43% (earlier data) to ~62% (later EPR-based data); FY2024-25 figure 13.97 lakh t by Zerodha article; a 14.14 lakh t FY2025-26 headline exists. Datasets conflict by definition and scope. — [DTE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/waste/indias-e-waste-surges-by-73-in-5-years), [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/indias-e-waste-recovery-jumps-past-70-a-compliance-turnaround-says-foxx-compliances-shashi-shekhar-shahi), [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their), [News Arena](https://newsarenaindia.com/nation/india-generated-14-14-lakh-mt-e-waste-in-2025-26-govt/71773)
- Embedded value: ~$6B in India's annual e-waste (Zerodha article); DTE headline "Rs 51,000 crore worth of materials" slips through system (headline only). — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their), [DTE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/waste/rs-51000-crore-worth-of-materials-in-indias-e-wastemost-slips-through-the-system)

### Inferences
- Opportunity: a branded "cash-competitive" buyback that prices the compressor and copper correctly and collects at doorstep. The wedge is that the same unit has a refurbish value above scrap value only if it passes triage (inference).
- Triage (refurb-grade vs harvest vs scrap) at the point of pickup is the missing step in current exchange programmes, which only do scrap-route valuation (inference from Attero-run valuation).
- No formal-channel advantage on price unless GST/RCM or EPR value is passed back to the consumer.

### Gaps
- No washing-machine scrap price source; no official price list; blog rates unverified.
- LG/Samsung actual exchange value tables not retrieved.
- No data on what fraction of old appliances are still working when traded in.

## 3. Refrigerant (HFC/HCFC) recovery, leakage, Kigali, climate credits

### Takeaway
India has no effective recovery/reclaim/destroy mandate in practice, ~40% of refrigerant demand is servicing (leakage), and end-of-life gas is typically vented informally. Credits for HFC recovery exist as drafts/US and Thai programmes but I found no adopted Article 5 (India) pathway.

### Cited Findings
- "India currently has no mandate to recover, reclaim, or destroy refrigerant gases effectively"; ~40% of India's refrigerant demand is servicing due to leakage; 18 mini-reclamation centres set up with multilateral fund support; refrigerants vented informally at end-of-life because of low resale value; cooling demand projected up ~8x by 2037-38. — [Refindustry](https://refindustry.com/news/market-research/current-status-and-barriers-to-lifecycle-refrigerant-management-in-india)
- E-Waste (Management) Second Amendment Rules 2023 (notified 24 July 2023) added refrigerant management at end-of-life for RAC under EPR; CEEW says implementation "yet to take up" (snippet-level). — [CEEW financing paper](https://www.ceew.in/publications/policy-mechanisms-for-financing-lifecycle-refrigerant-management-in-india) (page returned 403 to me; claim from search summary), [CEEW node 4560](https://www.ceew.in/node/4560)
- India ratified Kigali in 2021; HFC freeze from 2028 (some sources 2024 baseline then reductions from 2028), phase-down to 2047; the ODS Rules were to be amended for HFC control (notification status not confirmed by me). — [NRDC](https://www.nrdc.org/experts/anjali-jaiswal/india-takes-major-step-towards-climate-friendly-refrigerants), [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/blog/climate-change/differentiated-and-legally-binding-56094), [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/governance/modi-govt-commits-to-phasing-down-hfcs-greenhouse-gases-used-in-acs-and-fridges-by-2047/718381)
- >90% of new room ACs now use R-32 (NRDC, snippet-level). — [NRDC](https://www.nrdc.org/experts/anjali-jaiswal/india-takes-major-step-towards-climate-friendly-refrigerants)
- Project Drawdown: ~90% of refrigerant emissions occur at end of life; refrigerant management 57.15 Gt CO2e, net operating cost ~$622.7B (current technical summary; editions differ, 2017 book 89.7 Gt). — [Drawdown](https://drawdown.org/solutions/refrigerant-management)
- Carbon credits: Yale Carbon Containment Lab 2023 draft methodology for HFC recovery/destruction in Article 5 countries; academic paper notes no crediting mechanism for HFC destruction in Article 5 contexts; ACR HFC reclamation methodology and Thailand's T-VER exist; UNFCCC Article 6.4 draft comments debated reclaim vs destruction. — [Yale CC Lab](https://carboncontainmentlab.yale.edu/documents/yale-cc-lab-hfc-methodology-white-paper-may-2023-draft.pdf), [J-STAGE](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ger/29/2/29_29-2_08/_article/-char/en), [ACR](https://acrcarbon.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Performance-Standard-Supplement-HFC-v2.0.pdf), [UNFCCC A6.4](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/A6.4-INFO-MISC-009.pdf)
- UNEP Ozone Secretariat has an India lifecycle refrigerant management country presentation (not read). — [UNEP](https://ozone.unep.org/system/files/documents/Session-2_Country_experiences_with_LRM_India.pdf)

### Inferences
- A pickup service that touches the appliance before the kabadiwala (buyback) is the natural point to capture refrigerant, because recovery is cheapest while the sealed system is intact (inference).
- Credit value per fridge/AC is likely small (a few hundred grams to ~1 kg of refrigerant); the revenue case probably depends on EPR certificate value or OEM funding, not carbon credits alone. Not quantified (inference).
- Kigali-driven HFC freeze from 2028 may raise value of reclaimed HFCs for servicing (inference, no sourced price data).

### Gaps
- No refrigerant charge per unit, leakage rate, or Indian reclaimed-gas price from sourced documents.
- Whether HFC rules were finally notified and any recovery-specific guidelines under E-waste 2023 amendment: unconfirmed.
- CEEW paper contents unread (403).

## 4. EPR certificate market structure, arbitrage, fraud, small-producer compliance gap

### Takeaway
E-waste EPR is a certificate market with a price band and an active Delhi High Court challenge by major OEMs; certificate fraud is documented in plastic EPR (700k fake certificates) and verification gaps exist in e-waste, but I found no equivalent e-waste fraud dataset.

### Cited Findings
- Targets: 60% of historical sales now, 70% by FY2025-26, 80% by FY2027-28; CPCB EPR trading platform; certificates in kg, valid 2 years; ~Rs 10-50/kg in 2024-25 with Rs 22/kg floor from March 2024 amendment (single secondary source, verify against CPCB notification). — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- 2024 amendment: price band between 30% and 100% of environmental compensation. CPCB final environmental compensation guidelines 25 Aug 2025. — [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2024/03/11/decoding-the-amended-e-waste-management-rules.amp.html), [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/04/15/a-corporate-mutiny-threatens-indias-e-wonderland-dream)
- April 2025: Daikin, Samsung, LG, Havells, Voltas (and Carrier per another snippet) challenged floor price in Delhi HC; govt said price caps prevent a race to the bottom; case ongoing as of the sources. — [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/04/23/price-cap-on-e-waste-recycling-will-curb-irresponsible-disposal-modi-govt-to-delhi-hc), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/electronic-companies-market-forces-price-waste-delhi-hc-125080101783_1.html)
- Recycler economics: material sale alone not profitable; EPR certificate revenue makes recycling pay (plant official quote, snippet-level). — [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2025/04/15/a-corporate-mutiny-threatens-indias-e-wonderland-dream)
- Attero claims >30% EPR certificate share, nearest competitor under one-third its size. — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- Verification: the govt cannot reliably verify claimed recycling; producers earlier self-reported. — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- Fraud evidence is from PLASTIC EPR: CSE/CPCB found ~7 lakh fake certificates, claimed 6.9 lakh t recycled, 38x recyclers' capacity; CPCB inspections in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka; NGT suo motu. Counts vary (6-7 lakh). — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/waste/70k-fake-certificates-major-polluters-missing-misuse-of-epr-legislation-worsening-indias-plastic-problem-shows-cse-report), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/planet/how-indias-plastic-waste-rules-are-hollowed-out-by-loopholes-and-lax-enforcement)
- Lok Sabha Aug 2025 reply on fake invoices/unregistered vendors in e-waste: all producers, refurbishers, recyclers must register on CPCB portal. — [Sansad](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2994107/1/AU2361_mIpVFw.pdf) (snippet-level)
- Producer costs are visible in results: Bajaj Electricals EPR ~Rs 9.5 cr rising to ~Rs 18 cr; Havells reports EPR as line item. — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their)
- Refurbishers are a registered entity category under the 2022 rules; EPR for refurbishers/exchange not examined. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/waste-management/1374872/the-e-waste-management-rules-2022)

### Inferences
- Arbitrage exists when the floor/band is contested and certificates can be sourced from informal-fed recyclers with weak traceability; this is analogous to the plastic fraud but unproven for e-waste (inference).
- A traceable refurb/buyback operator could generate certificate-backed or OEM-funded revenue per unit collected (inference).

### Gaps
- No data on small producer/importer non-compliance rates or enforcement actions in e-waste.
- No Delhi HC final outcome found; check as of Nov 2026.
- No e-waste-specific certificate fraud counts.

## 5. Informal recycler health and safety, women/child labour, just transition, NGO/CSR models

### Takeaway
Informal workers carry severe health exposure and are poorly protected, and formalisation is already squeezing their income without a transition plan. Inclusion models exist mostly as analogues; CSR-funded India e-waste pilots were not found.

### Cited Findings
- 76% of informal e-waste workers suffer respiratory ailments; an estimated 4-5 lakh children aged 10-15 in e-waste handling (secondary citations). — [Zerodha Chatter](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_waste_in_India)
- Seelampur, Delhi: women and children paid least; Rs 200-800/day; women burn cables over coal fires for copper. — [Eco-Business](https://www.eco-business.com/news/electronic-waste-is-recycled-in-appalling-conditions-in-india/), [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/connecting-the-unconnected/delhis-unregulated-e-waste-markets-and-the-marginalised-women-workers-who-bear-its-cost)
- E-waste workers sit outside the Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008 scheme through "legal invisibility". — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/connecting-the-unconnected/delhis-unregulated-e-waste-markets-and-the-marginalised-women-workers-who-bear-its-cost)
- 2026 Frontiers study of 427 women waste pickers in Tamil Nadu links more contact with hazardous waste to higher health risk. — [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainability/articles/10.3389/frsus.2026.1833366/full)
- Seelampur workers losing business as formal recyclers expand and home dismantling is restricted; formal recyclers depend on informal workers but cannot absorb them. — [Context/TRF](https://www.context.news/just-transition/in-delhis-e-waste-hub-indias-informal-workers-lose-business), [Malay Mail](https://malaymail.com/news/money/2025/11/27/indias-e-waste-crackdown-is-growing-but-it-is-squeezing-the-workers-who-built-the-system/199751)
- Inclusion analogues (not Indian e-waste): Ghana 230 informal collectors trained and integrated; Gurugram Alag Karo dry-waste centre. ORF argues formalisation without social transformation is unjust. — [CSE/Rachna Arora deck](https://cdn.cseindia.org/attachments/0.84545800_1754469303_rachna-arora.pdf), [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/just-transition-for-informal-waste-workers-in-the-circular-economy)
- UNDP/GEF (MeitY) project to pilot rebate schemes and gender-responsive upgrading. — [UNDP](https://www.undp.org/india/press-releases/india-advances-transition-circular-economy-electronics-sector-gef-and-undp-support)

### Inferences
- Large-appliance refurb/harvest roles (testing, board repair, compressor handling) could absorb dismantlers into safer, higher-paid skilled work, similar to Costify's ITI-trained technician model (inference).
- CSR/ESG funding fit is plausible but unverified here.

### Gaps
- No CSR budget figures, no named Indian e-waste NGO programme (Hasiru Dala/GIZ/Toxics Link pilots not found); no CEEW worker study retrieved.

## 6. Product-as-a-service, rental economics, landlords, second-life in low-income markets

### Takeaway
Rentomojo shows rental plus in-house refurbishment can be profitable in India and assets live ~10 years; large-appliance specifics (washing machine/fridge share) are not broken out.

### Cited Findings
- Rentomojo FY26: revenue Rs 387 cr (+45.5%), PAT Rs 104.3 cr incl. one-time Rs 36.6 cr tax credit; depreciation Rs 70 cr; logistics Rs 28 cr, contract manpower Rs 38 cr, refurbishment Rs 19 cr, marketing Rs 21 cr; 8.51 lakh live items, 83.34% rented; 2.54 lakh subscribers in 29 cities; useful life ~10 years; 2017 assets have earned 5.12x original cost; 56% of 2017 assets still earning. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/rentomojos-post-ipo-test-can-the-flywheel-keep-going/)
- Market: Redseer estimates furniture and appliance rental Rs 350 cr (2021) to Rs 1,550 cr (2025) to ~Rs 6,030 cr (2030); Rentomojo 42-47% organised share. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/rentomojos-post-ipo-test-can-the-flywheel-keep-going/)
- FY25: Rentomojo revenue Rs 266 cr, profit Rs 43 cr; refurbishment workforce of 1,688 technicians/carpenters/painters (snippet-level). — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/rentomojo-posts-rs-266-cr-revenue-in-fy25-profits-surge-92-10930380), [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/12/rentomojo-sees-profit-spike-to-rs-43-cr-in-fy25-on-subscription-led-growth)
- Furlenco: FY24 revenue ~Rs 140 cr (figures vary 139.6-151.9), FY25 reportedly Rs 240 cr with Rs 3 cr profit (Tracxn via Mint, unverified); owns inventory so higher depreciation; reportedly eyeing IPO H2 2027. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/furlenco-posts-rs-140-cr-revenue-in-fy24-finance-cost-shrink-52-7564005), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/rentomojos-post-ipo-test-can-the-flywheel-keep-going/)
- Rentomojo's pre-IPO analyses cite contribution margin >60% and EBITDA ~40%+ (unverified against DRHP). — [IPOCentral](https://ipocentral.in/rentomojo-ipo-analysis-how-rentomojo-makes-money/)

### Inferences
- Rental provides a captive refurbishment feedstock and justifies refurb capacity; end-of-rental-life units (year 8-10) are a supply of second-life, harvestable and recyclable units (inference).
- Landlord leasing (furnished-rental bundles) and low-income-market second life: I did not find sourced evidence.

### Gaps
- No appliance-only rental economics; no landlord leasing data; no low-income second-life/export evidence.

## 7. Recycling technology maturity (robotic disassembly, AI sorting, urban mining)

### Takeaway
Robotic disassembly is proven only for high-volume uniform products (iPhones). I found nothing on large-appliance robotic disassembly or AI sorting maturity.

### Cited Findings
- Apple's Daisy disassembles ~200 iPhones/hour across 15 models; Gizmodo at launch called it a prototype (one unit). — [Ceramics.org](https://ceramics.org/ceramic-tech-today/apples-new-robot-daisy-recycles-high-quality-materials-from-up-to-200-devices-per-hour), [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/apple-s-e-waste-problem-will-take-more-than-robots-to-s-1825400535)
- India wants to extract critical minerals from e-waste (policy interest). — [Context](https://www.context.news/?p=90683)

### Inferences
- Appliance variety and low value per unit make robotic disassembly uneconomic in India given labour costs (inference).

### Gaps
- No sources for AI-based sorting, appliance disassembly lines, or urban-mining unit economics.
