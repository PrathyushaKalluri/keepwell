## 1. Refurbished/recommerce players (Back Market, refurbed, Cashify, Reebelo, Swappie, Underdog, Zefo/Quikr) and market size

### Takeaway
Back Market is the scale leader (about EUR3.5B GMV in 2025, EBITDA-positive per some sources, last priced at $5.7B in 2022). Cashify is the India leader, near break-even in FY25 and preparing a roughly Rs1,800 cr IPO. Phones dominate recommerce; major-appliance refurbishment is a much smaller niche (Underdog, France).

### Inferences
- Phone recommerce works because of high residual value, fast replacement cycles (about 2.5 years), standardised grading and easy shipping. Appliances are heavy, low-residual-value and costly to transport, so unit economics are weaker (inference; no appliance-specific source found).
- Indian recommerce is being re-rated as an IPO story only after loss reduction; Cashify is the template.

## 2. Rental/subscription (Rentomojo, Furlenco, Cityfurnish, Grover, Bundles)

### Takeaway
Indian furniture/appliance rental turned profitable in FY25 after years of losses (Rentomojo third profitable year; Furlenco first profit). Grover needed restructuring and a recapitalisation. Bundles (NL) was not researched.

### Cited Findings
- Rentomojo FY25: revenue about Rs266 cr (vs Rs193 cr, +38%); net profit Rs43.07 cr (vs Rs22.49 cr, +92%); third consecutive profitable year; IPO-bound. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/rentomojo-posts-rs-266-cr-revenue-in-fy25-profits-surge-92-10930380); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/12/rentomojo-sees-profit-spike-to-rs-43-cr-in-fy25-on-subscription-led-growth)
- Furlenco (House of Kieraya): FY25 operating revenue about Rs229 cr (+64-65%); profit about Rs3.1 cr vs Rs130 cr loss in FY24; spent Rs1.03 per Rs1 of revenue vs Rs2.03 a year earlier. Raised about Rs125 cr equity from Sheela Foam and WhiteOak (reported Dec 2025); mulling about Rs100 cr pre-IPO round. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/furlenco-turns-around-in-fy25-posts-profit-after-rs-130-cr-loss-revenue-surges-65-10586639); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/furlenco-turns-profitable-in-fy25-revenue-zooms-64-to-inr-229-cr); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/furlenco-secures-125-cr-equity-from-sheela-foam-whiteoak-for-expansion-aims-for-ipo-after-fy25-profit)
- Cityfurnish: FY25 revenue about Rs36.6 cr (+11.9%), PAT about Rs4 lakh (Inc42, with year-labelling inconsistencies); total funding about $7.5-12.3M depending on source; $2.5M debt in Nov 2022. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/cityfurnish/financials/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/cityfurnish/financials)
- Grover (Germany): Series A EUR37M (2019), $250M debt/equity deal Jan 2022 (unicorn-reported), EUR50M bridge in Jul 2024, further recapitalisation Mar 2025 after strategic review and restructuring (Augmentum added EUR3.5M); leadership team completed Dec 2025. Total raised figures conflict (ZoomInfo about $2.3B vs Multiples $284M equity). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1680691); [Grover press](https://press.grover.com/136970-grover-rings-in-the-new-year-with-250m-funding-deal); [Startbase](https://www.startbase.com/news/grover-sichert-sich-weitere-50-millionen-euro/)
- Servify (device after-sales/protection, India): $65M Series D 2022 (Singularity Growth), pre-IPO and acquisitive; Rs66.27 cr Series D3 (about $7.8M) in 2025 led by BEENEXT, partly to repay debt; last acquisition on aggregator is Jubi.ai (Jan 2023). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/23/apple-samsung-partner-servify-65-million-usd-series-d-funding-round-public-ipo-plans); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/servify-secures-inr-6627-cr-in-series-d3-funding-led-by/488839)

### Inferences
- Rental profitability in India followed cost discipline and asset-light/owned-capex discipline (Furlenco cost ratio halved), not growth; Grover's trajectory shows rental is capital-hungry and rate-sensitive.

## 4. Business-model lessons

### Takeaway
Evidence retrieved supports: profitability followed cost-ratio reductions (Furlenco, Cashify losses -80%); capital-heavy rental needs restructuring when funding tightens (Grover).

### Cited Findings
- Furlenco cost per rupee of revenue fell from Rs2.03 to Rs1.03; Cashify loss fell about 80% — see Entrackr links above.
- Homejoy (US home services) shut in 2015 citing inability to raise and labour lawsuits — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2015/07/17/homejoy-closing-cleaning-google/) (analogue only, not the Indian Housejoy).

### Inferences
- Marketplaces with a trust problem (hidden electronics faults) need warranties and grading, which is why Underdog sells with 2-year warranties.
- Home-services marketplaces are fragile due to leakage and labour classification (from Homejoy analogue).

- No primary-source analysis of failed Indian after-sales startups; lessons are thin and should be supplemented.
