# Global extended-warranty, device/appliance protection and home-warranty industry (as of Nov 2026)

Research note: the web search tool was rate-limited and several searches returned no results. Primary-source SEC and press-release pages were seen only through search summaries, not fetched in full. Unit economics (loss ratios, commissions, attach rates) and several companies (AIG/Amtrust, Allianz Partners, Onsitego, Zopper, Bajaj Allianz, Asurion audited revenue) are largely unsourced and are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Leading players: scale, revenue, M&A, regulation, litigation

### Takeaway
Asurion is the largest private device/appliance protection player and has just bought Domestic & General (UK) for a reported GBP 2.1bn. Assurant (Connected Living), Allstate Protection Plans and Frontdoor are public and growing mid-to-high single digits or better. Enforcement is mostly state-level (home warranty) and FTC/state against auto service contracts, not against appliance plans.

## 4. Right to repair and EU legal guarantees

### Takeaway
Directive (EU) 2024/1799 adds 12 months to the legal guarantee when a seller chooses repair, for contracts from 31 July 2026. This is a mild headwind for paid extended cover but not a ban.

### Cited Findings
- Seller liability is extended once by 12 months when repair is the remedy within the legal guarantee; applies to the whole product, once only; member states may go longer; does not apply to contracts before 31 July 2026; sellers must tell buyers before repair. — [European Parliament](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240419IPR20590/right-to-repair-making-repair-easier-and-more-appealing-to-consumers); [TLT](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/from-replacement-to-repair-what-the-eu-right-to-repair-directive-means-for-businesses)
- Transposition: reported Germany in force 23 Jul 2026, Italy decree 176/2026 in force 22 Oct 2026 (single roundup; verify nationally). — [Pasquale Pillitteri roundup](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/26277/right-to-repair-europe-country-by-country)
- Euronews (8 Sep 2026) frames potential consumer savings of EUR 12bn on appliance repairs. — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/08/can-the-eu-save-consumers-12bn-on-appliance-repairs)

### Inferences
- The EU statutory guarantee is already 2 years; repairs extend it to 3 effectively, which overlaps the first year of typical paid extended warranties and may erode attach there. It could also help repair-network players. This is inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No empirical data on the effect on extended-warranty demand; no US right-to-repair (state laws, FTC) coverage found in this pass.

### Inferences
