## EU: Directive (EU) 2024/1799 details, 31 July 2026 application, transposition status

### Takeaway
The Directive was adopted 13 June 2024, and the national rules had to apply from 31 July 2026. By autumn 2026 transposition was clearly patchy: Germany completed it, while Poland, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands missed the deadline as of early Sep 2026. One source reports Commission formal notices to 18 states on 25 Sep 2026, but that claim is secondary and uncorroborated.

### Cited Findings
- Adopted 13 June 2024; applies from 31 July 2026. Obligations: manufacturers of Annex II products must repair within a reasonable time and for a reasonable price; no contract, hardware or software barriers to repair without legitimate objective justification; reasonable pricing of spare parts the manufacturer supplies; publish repair info and indicative prices of typical repairs on a free website; +1 year legal guarantee extension if the consumer picks repair over replacement (amends Directive 2019/771); each Member State must adopt at least one repair-promotion measure (vouchers, campaigns, training); optional European Repair Information Form (Annex I) whose conditions stay valid 30 days. — [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/directive-repair-goods_en)
- Annex II was extended to domestic local space heaters from 31 July 2026 via Delegated Directive (EU) 2026/74 of 12 January 2026; Annex II is updated as new product repairability requirements are adopted (e.g., fridges and smartphones are listed). — [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/directive-repair-goods_en)
- Entry into force: one source says 30 July 2024, another 31 July 2024 [minor conflict; the 30 July date appears in the Right to Repair Europe/Cleolabs and Commission Q&A material]. — [Cleolabs](https://www.cleolabs.co/en/blog/eu-right-to-repair-transposition-2026); [Commission Q&A memo](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/2d443b31-dc2a-4e54-a9ec-5fbb61c895e9_en?filename=2024_07_18_QA_Right2repair_FINAL.pdf)
- Germany: Bundestag first reading 20 May 2026; Reparaturgesetz passed 26 June 2026 per Cleolabs, 25 Jun 2026 per myDPP [date conflict by one day]; published in the Federal Law Gazette, in force 31 July 2026. — [Cleolabs](https://www.cleolabs.co/en/blog/eu-right-to-repair-transposition-2026); [myDPP, status as of 8 Sep 2026](https://mydpp.app/en/knowledge/right-to-repair-national-transposition-status)
- Poland: not transposed as of 8 Sep 2026; UOKiK bill UC154 published 15 Jun 2026, Council of Ministers adoption planned Q3 2026, in force end of 2026 at earliest. Spain: draft within planned Ley de Consumo Sostenible, TRIS-notified, consultation to 15 Sep 2026, likely 2027. Italy: draft legislative decree approved preliminarily 2 Jul 2026, sent to chambers 9 Jul 2026. Netherlands: bill submitted to Tweede Kamer 10 Jul 2026, expected end of 2026. — [myDPP](https://mydpp.app/en/knowledge/right-to-repair-national-transposition-status)
- Reported (secondary, single source in search summary) that on 25 Sep 2026 the Commission sent letters of formal notice to 18 states for incomplete transposition of the Directive and of delegated Directive 2026/74; France, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal named; Italy reportedly completed on 7 Oct 2026. [UNVERIFIED: I could not retrieve the underlying page; myDPP page itself says formal notices "typically follow within a few months" and does not confirm the 18 count.] — via search summary of [Cleolabs](https://www.cleolabs.co/en/blog/eu-right-to-repair-transposition-2026) / [Spilma](https://www.spilma.com/en/actualites/right-to-repair-directive-transposition)
- Member-state repair-promotion measure notification date reported as 31 July 2029 [UNVERIFIED, single secondary source]. — [Spilma](https://www.spilma.com/en/actualites/right-to-repair-directive-transposition)

### Inferences
- Legal obligations bite via national law, so a brand's compliance depends on which of the 27 transpositions apply; expect uneven enforcement into 2027.
- Italy, Netherlands, Poland and Spain are the near-term transposition watch list.

### Gaps
- No official Commission transposition tracker (EUR-Lex NIM database) was reached; no confirmed status for France, Austria, Belgium, Ireland etc.
- Confirmation of the 25 Sep 2026 formal-notice batch needs a Commission infringement press release.

## EU online repair platform date (2026 vs 2028 conflict)

### Takeaway
The conflict is resolved by distinguishing steps: 31 July 2026 is the date Member States notify their national contact points or platforms; 31 July 2027 is the Article 7 deadline for the Commission to build the common online interface; the Commission's own page expects the platform operational in January 2028. No source supports a 2026 launch.

### Cited Findings
- Article 7 platform: Commission builds common interface by 31 July 2027; Member States must use it for national sections; Member States notify national contact point/platforms by 31 July 2026; report on application by 31 July 2031. — [Circular Cities and Regions (EC) summary](https://circular-cities-and-regions.ec.europa.eu/support-materials/eu-regulations-legislation/directive-eu-20241799-repair-goods); article text mirrored at [gesetze.legal Art. 7](https://gesetze.legal/eu/rl_2024_1799_eu/7)
- Commission page: platform to be an extension of the "Your Europe" portal, expected operational in January 2028; Commission builds IT infrastructure, Member States register repairers. — [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/directive-repair-goods_en)

### Inferences
- "2026" in some sources probably refers to the notification/application date; "2028" matches the Commission's operational estimate. The statutory build date (2027) sits between.

### Gaps
- Exact Art. 7 wording was not read directly on EUR-Lex; the 2027 date comes from an EC-hosted summary.

## EU ecodesign (appliance spare parts), ESPR, digital product passport

### Takeaway
Since March 2021 the ecodesign regulations for washing machines/washer-dryers, dishwashers, refrigerating appliances and displays impose spare-part availability of roughly 7-10 years (professional repairers), with 15-working-day delivery. ESPR digital product passports are not yet binding for any product group; appliance-specific acts are not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Washing machines and washer-dryers (Reg. (EU) 2019/2023): minimum 10 years of spare-part availability, delivery within 15 working days. — [Commission Q&A 19/5889](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/qanda_19_5889/QANDA_19_5889_EN.pdf)
- Package overall 7-10 years minimum; e.g., 7 years for refrigerating appliances, 10 years for dishwashers (per press summary; part-by-part periods vary, some parts 7 years); access to certain parts and repair info restricted to professional repairers; max delivery time 3 weeks criticized by ANEC/BEUC. Covers five product types (lighting, fridges, TV screens, dishwashers, washing machines). — [BEUC](https://www.beuc.eu/publications/eu-makes-5-household-appliances-safer-and-repairable-does-not-go-full-mile); [Telegraph via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/the-daily-telegraph/20210302/282084869534957)
- Regulation numbers: from my knowledge, 2019/2019 refrigerating appliances, 2019/2021 electronic displays, 2019/2022 dishwashers, 2019/2023 washing machines. A search summary mis-assigned some numbers; the 2019/2023 washing machine number is confirmed by the Commission Q&A above, the others [UNVERIFIED in this session]. Per-product TV availability years not retrieved.
- Smartphones/tablets (separate regime, applies from 20 June 2025): critical parts within 5-10 working days, 7 years after end of sale; 5 years of software updates. — [Cullen International](https://www.cullen-international.com/news/2025/05/New-ecodesign-requirements-for-smartphones-and-tablets-to-apply-this-month.html)
- DPP/ESPR: first ESPR working plan adopted 16 April 2025 (COM(2025) 187); iron and steel first, delegated act expected Q4 2026; textiles/tyres/aluminium ~2027; furniture ~2028; mattresses ~2029; each act applies at least 18 months after adoption; as of mid-2026 no product-specific delegated act adopted. A vendor blog lists dishwashers and washing machines as indicative 2026 candidates, other sources do not [CONTESTED/weak]. — [Renoon](https://www.renoon.com/blog/the-timeline-of-digital-product-passport-regulation-in-eu-espr-from-product-groups-to-rollout-schedule); [Renoon status](https://www.renoon.com/blog/delegated-acts-for-digital-product-passport-explainer-and-current-status)
- JRC is running ecodesign/ESPR preparatory stakeholder meetings (documents dated Nov 2025 and Apr 2026), which suggests a washing-machine-type revision is in preparation [inference from file titles only]. — [JRC 2026-04 deck](https://susproc.jrc.ec.europa.eu/product-bureau/sites/default/files/2026-04/2nd%20Stakeholder%20meeting%20PPT_website.pdf)

### Inferences
- Appliance DPP is unlikely to bind before about 2028-2029.

### Gaps
- Exact per-part availability years for TVs and fridges, and EUR-Lex text, not retrieved. Need the Commission working plan to confirm appliance ESPR timing.

## EU national schemes (France, Austria, Germany)

### Takeaway
France runs the QualiRépar bonus (large appliances about 50 EUR) and replaced the repairability index with a durability index for washing machines from 8 Apr 2025. Austria's Reparaturbonus ran dry in May 2025 and was replaced by a narrower "Geräte-Retter-Prämie" in 2026, whose continuation is uncertain.

### Cited Findings
- France: bonus appears as a flat discount on the bill at a labelled repairer; most sources say 50 EUR for washing machine/dishwasher/dryer; one says cap 60 EUR for large appliances [CONTESTED; official ecosystem/QualiRépar schedule not read]. — [Selectra](https://selectra.info/energie/actualites/marche/bonus-reparation-lave-linge-50-euros-qualirepar); [Wizbii](https://money.wizbii.com/aide/bonus-reparation-guide-complet)
- France: durability index (indice de durabilité) replaces repairability index for washing machines, effective 8 April 2025; adds robustness, reliability, software updates. — [info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/avant-dacheter-consultez-lindice-de-durabilite-des-produits)
- Austria: original Reparaturbonus covered 50% up to 200 EUR, ended 26 May 2025 when funds ran out; over 130 million EUR spent since 2022; relaunched as Geräte-Retter-Prämie, cap 130 EUR, mostly household appliances (bikes, e-bikes, phones dropped), planned budget about 30 million EUR/yr 2026-2028; start reported 12 January 2026. Status is conflicting: a 15 June 2026 Salzburg24 headline reports an end due to cuts, while a 30 Oct 2026 piece calls it the "last chance" before a deadline [CONTESTED]. Vienna has a separate Wiener Reparaturbon. — [The International](https://www.theinternational.at/austria-replaces-repair-bonus-with-new-device-saver-program/); [Salzburg24](https://www.salzburg24.at/news/oesterreich/letzte-chance-auf-reparaturbonus-frist-fuer-geraete-retter-praemie-endet-bald-art-343040); [WKO](https://www.wko.at/ktn/handel/elektro-einrichtungsfachhandel/geraete-retter-praemie-startet-im-jaenner-2026-neu-durch)
- Germany: Reparaturgesetz in force 31 Jul 2026 (see above). Berlin reportedly keeps its repair bonus [weak source]. — [particlenews](https://particlenews.pages.dev/story/berlin-keeps-repair-bonus-as-austria-sets-out-narrower-device-saver-scheme)

### Gaps
- No federal German repair voucher scheme found; Thuringia/Berlin local schemes not verified. French bonus 2026 official schedule and funding (eco-organism Ecosystem) not retrieved.

## US: state laws, federal/FTC

### Takeaway
Roughly 13 states have some right-to-repair statute (July 2026 count), but most are electronics-focused. Connecticut is the clearest appliance law (products from 1 July 2026); New York exempts home appliances with an embedded digital product; there is no federal law, and the FTC action that settled was against Deere (farm equipment), not appliances.

### Cited Findings
- State table (Faegre Drinker, 30 Jul 2026): California consumer electronics; Colorado electronics (retroactive to products from 1 Jul 2021); Connecticut "electronic or appliance products" from 1 Jul 2026, including major home appliances; Kansas signed 9 Apr 2026, effective 1 Jul 2027 (digital electronics over $50 wholesale); Minnesota digital electronics (products since 1 Jul 2021), excludes medical/agri/auto; New York digital electronic equipment made after 1 Jul 2023, home appliances with embedded digital product exempt; Oregon electronics after 1 Jul 2025, penalties from 1 Jul 2027, parts pairing prohibited; Texas effective 1 Sep 2026; Washington electronics law effective 1 Jan 2026 and prohibits parts pairing in three respects; Maine/Massachusetts vehicles; Maryland/Nevada wheelchairs/mobility. — [Faegre Drinker, 30 Jul 2026](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/7/unlocking-the-right-to-repair-the-expanding-compliance-landscape)
- Count disagreement: Faegre says 13; PIRG called Washington the 8th state earlier; H2 Compliance lists Texas as 8th [CONTESTED, definitional]. — [PIRG](https://pirg.org/updates/washington-becomes-eighth-state-with-right-to-repair-law-on-the-books/); [H2 Compliance](https://h2compliance.com/texas-right-to-repair/)
- Connecticut SB 3 (Public Act 25-44): documentation, parts and tools on fair and reasonable terms to owners and independent shops; AG-only enforcement, no CUTPA private right; resource-duration tiers of 3 years for $50-$100 products and 5 years above $100 [UNVERIFIED tiers; statute ch. 735b not read]. — [CBIA](https://www.cbia.com/news/manufacturing/consumer-protection-bill); [CT General Assembly](https://www.cga.ct.gov/2026/sup/chap_735b.htm)
- Washington HB 1483 reportedly covers electronics and appliances per one source, while Faegre's table says appliances "not addressed" [CONTESTED]. — [ServiceMag](https://www.servicemag.org/news/right-to-repair-laws-2026)
- Federal: no enacted law. Fair Repair Act H.R. 7404 introduced 5 Feb 2026 (documentation, parts, tools; parts-pairing ban; FTC enforcement), referred to Energy and Commerce; narrower automotive H.R. 7389 advanced 48-1 on 21 May 2026. — [PolicyRisk H.R. 7404](https://policyrisk.com/research/legislation/HR7404); [Faegre](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/7/unlocking-the-right-to-repair-the-expanding-compliance-landscape)
- FTC v. Deere settled 8 Jul 2026 (10-year agreement); private Deere class settlement $99M with preliminary approval May 2026; June 2026 presidential memorandum "Lowering the Cost of Living by Promoting the Freedom to Fix" led to EPA guidance on 1 Jul 2026 (vehicles/equipment). — [Faegre](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/7/unlocking-the-right-to-repair-the-expanding-compliance-landscape)
- Appliance evidence base (Feb 2023): PIRG/iFixit/Repair.org surveyed 50 makers and 37 technicians; about 9 in 10 makers refuse to share repair manuals even with certified pros; LG and Samsung worst, Miele and Bosch tied second; GE SmartHQ documentation costs $919; 27% of technicians say their company pays over $1,000/yr for documentation; all but one technician report at least occasional trouble getting parts. — [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/News/71693/appliance-repair-pros-struggle-to-find-lg-and-samsung-documentation)

### Gaps
- California SB 244 (2023) covers products with 3-7 year parts availability depending on price, but appliance scope not verified here. No FTC action on appliance OEMs found. Colorado/Oregon appliance coverage unconfirmed.

## India

### Takeaway
No binding right-to-repair statute exists as of my findings; India has a voluntary Right to Repair Portal (DoCA, launched Dec 2022) and a committee-driven Repairability Index framework, with smartphones/tablets as phase one. Consumer durables were named an initial focus sector but nothing binding found for them.

### Cited Findings
- DoCA committee set up July 2022; initial sectors farming equipment, mobile/tablets, consumer durables, automobiles; Right to Repair Portal launched Dec 2022 with brand-provided warranty and post-sale info. — [India.gov.in portal](https://www.india.gov.in/right-repair-portal-india); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/govt-sets-up-committee-to-develop-framework-on-right-to-repair/1038720/)
- Sept 2024 committee chaired by Bharat Khera developed the Repairability Index; May 2025 reporting that it picked smartphones and tablets for phase one, OEM self-declaration and mandatory display at point of sale; six core parameters including disassembly depth and repair information; later phases may extend to laptops etc. — [Business Today, 5 May 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/govt-to-introduce-a-repairability-index-for-smartphones-and-tablets-474723-2025-05-05); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/industry/india-to-introduce-repairability-index-for-electronics-to-combat-e-waste-crisis)
- Right to repair is not a statutory right in India; a CCI ruling is cited. — [Legal Service India](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/?p=34227)
- Market structure: unorganised spare-part trade with no traceability; Nehru Place known for third-party repair; small shops disadvantaged in obtaining OEM parts (Samsung, LG, Apple); Toxics Link (Mar 2026) recommends certification and better genuine-parts access; 1 in 2 households reportedly cannot repair white goods due to cost. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/connect/the-repair-gap-indias-apple-market-cannot-afford-to-ignore-4129808); [Toxics Link, Mar 2026](https://toxicslink.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Electronic%20Repair%20report.pdf); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/why-hyderabads-it-household-repair-shops-have-become-bad-at-fixing-your-devices-study); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/why-india-needs-right-to-repair-1-in-2-households-unable-to-repair-whitegoods-and-appliances-due-to-prohibitive-cost/67798.html)
- Urban Company states washing-machine technicians buy parts themselves and LG parts are sourced from the OEM. — [Urban Company](https://urbancompany.com/delhi-ncr-washing-machine-repair-sector-82-faridabad)

### Gaps
- No 2026 DoCA notification, rule or committee report found; portal launch date (2022 vs 2024) conflicts across sources. Lajpat Rai Market (Delhi) and online parts sellers in India not found. BIS/e-waste (EPR) sectoral frameworks not researched.

## Spare-parts marketplaces and companies

### Takeaway
The US is dominated by distributors and DIY e-tailers (Marcone/mSupply, Encompass/Parts Town, PartSelect, RepairClinic, Appliance Parts Pros); in France SOS Accessoire has consolidated by M&A. Ownership details for several US names were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- SOS Accessoire (France): acquired Miss Pièces (Apr 2023), the Dragon group (second French online B2C parts seller, strong on marketplaces), and Iris group (SDS France, Vedelec Belgium; Iris 62 M EUR revenue in 2023) in Dec 2024; combined group about 120 M EUR revenue and almost 300 staff per Berrylaw. Own revenue figures conflict: 25 M EUR (2022, BusinessCoot), 44 M EUR (CF News), 17.57 M EUR (2021 filing) [CONTESTED, different dates/perimeters]. — [Leader Réunion](https://www.leaderreunion.fr/en/la-reparation-delectromenager-donne-naissance-a-un-leader/); [Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/deals/96137-berrylaw-conseille-sos-accessoire-lors-du-build-up-realise-sur-le-groupe-iris.html); [BusinessCoot](https://www.businesscoot.com/en/company/sos-accessoires)
- Spareka: founded 2012, Montreuil; a competitor of SOS Accessoire; no revenue or acquisition found. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/spareka)
- Marcone rebranded to mSupply in March 2025; distributor of appliance, HVAC, plumbing, pool/spa parts; acquired Munch's Supply 2021; older press release: 122 facilities, about 2,100 staff, about 43,000 technician customers [dated]. — [Distribution Strategy](https://distributionstrategy.com/2025/05/why-marcone-rebranded-to-msupply-and-diversified/)
- PartSelect says it is a division of Eldis Group (since 1945); RepairClinic and Appliance Parts Pros ownership not confirmed. Encompass is linked to PT Holdings/Parts Town (Parts Town Home includes Encompass brand). — [PartSelect About](https://www.partselect.com/about/); [Encompass](https://solutions.encompass.com/tag/repair-parts)
- Market size (vendor reports, low reliability): global home appliance parts and accessories about $45.7B in 2024 to $78.9B by 2034 (5.5% CAGR) per Emergen Research; Market Research Future gives $33.4B for washing-machine parts alone, which looks inconsistent [CONTESTED]; India appliance repair service about $981M in 2023 (BlueWeave). — [Emergen](https://www.emergenresearch.com/index.php/industry-report/home-appliance-parts-accessories-market); [Market Research Future](https://marketresearchfuture.com/reports/washing-machine-parts-market-22931); [BlueWeave](https://blueweaveconsulting.com/report/india-home-appliance-repair-service-market)

Note manufacturer-aligned sources.
- Parts pairing: Colorado, Oregon, Washington ban it in statute (see US); H.R. 7404 would ban federally. Appliance-specific serialization/calibration practices by Samsung/LG/Bosch not documented in sources found; one repair blog mentions LG ThinQ re-pairing after board swap [anecdotal]. — [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com/News/71693/appliance-repair-pros-struggle-to-find-lg-and-samsung-documentation)

### Inferences
- Both EU and US rules target pricing and access (fair/reasonable terms), so independent parts distributors gain access leverage; consolidation (SOS Accessoire, mSupply) suggests scale economics.
