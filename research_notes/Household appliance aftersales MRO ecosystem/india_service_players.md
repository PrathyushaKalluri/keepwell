# India household-appliance after-sales service: marketplaces, OEM networks, aggregators (as of Nov 2026)

Note: Many figures come from search-result summaries of secondary sources, not primary filings. Items flagged UNVERIFIED conflict across sources or have no primary source.

## Urban Company (appliance repair/AC/RO, financials, listing, Native, disintermediation)

### Takeaway
Urban Company is the dominant listed home-services marketplace. Revenue is growing about 36-44% a year, but the company swung from FY25 profit to FY26 loss because of its InstaHelp quick-service push. Appliance-repair and AC category economics are not disclosed in anything I found. Native (own-brand RO purifiers) is a fast-growing but small hardware line. Disintermediation is a flagged risk.

### Inferences
- Native converts UC's RO-service base into hardware plus a captive filter and service annuity. It also hedges disintermediation, since a 2-3 year no-service warranty ties the customer to UC.
- InstaHelp spend, not the core marketplace, is driving the losses.

### Gaps
- No category-level NTV or GMV split for appliance repair, AC or RO. Check the RHP (Goldman-hosted copy dated 2 Sep 2025) and shareholder letters.
- No UC technician count or appliance-repair share. Q2 FY27 Native figures come from search snippets; confirm in the NSE letter.
- FY25 profit of Rs 239.8 cr likely includes a one-off (tax or deferred-tax); I did not verify this.

## Other marketplaces and aggregators: alive, acquired or shut

### Takeaway
Servify, OneAssist, Zopper and Onsitego are the B2B2C after-sales and warranty-services players. They are alive, with revenue of Rs 400-780 cr, and much of it is device protection rather than appliance repair. Housejoy, Jeeves and Mr Right/Repairwale are legacy or unverified. Quick-service players (Snabbit, Pronto) are well-funded but focus on chores, not appliance repair.

### Cited Findings

Protection plans were about 97% of operating income. ## OEM service networks, complaints and consumer-court trends

### Takeaway
Only Samsung gave a recent, company-sourced network size. Consumer durables is consistently a top-5 sector on the National Consumer Helpline, ranking second in 2024. Consumer commissions routinely hold both OEM and seller liable for failed warranty repairs.

### Cited Findings
- Samsung India: 3,000+ service touchpoints (centres, resident engineers, collection points), 12,500+ trained engineers (Samsung's own Dec 2025 line says 12,000), 16 parts warehouses. Dost skilling programme with 22 ITIs has trained 14,500+ engineers. — [Samsung India newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/in/from-logbooks-to-ai-powered-care-samsung-marks-30-years-of-customer-service-in-india); [TelecomLead](https://telecomlead.com/smart-phone/samsung-marks-30-years-of-customer-service-in-india-with-ai-powered-support-and-3000-service-touchpoints-125999)
- National Consumer Helpline (NCH), consumer durables rank and complaints: 2022 5th with 49,066; 2023 4th with 38,885; 2024 2nd with 77,873 (behind e-commerce at 440,185). Average monthly NCH complaints rose from 37,062 (2017) to 111,951 (2024). — [Rajya Sabha PQ 267, 18 Mar 2025](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/759128/1/PQ_267_18032025_U1951_p192_p193.pdf)
- NCH dashboards list sector refunds and company-wise grievances (for example Haier Appliances India). — [NCH consumer durables refund report](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/dashboard/companywiserefund/5)
- Consumer commission cases show the pattern: Delhi East commission ordered Fujitsu General to refund Rs 1.14 lakh plus 7% interest and Rs 25,000 compensation for a defective AC (Apr 2026). Hisar held Lloyd and its seller liable. Jaipur held LG and its dealer liable for failure to repair ACs under warranty. There is also a consumer case against Urban Clap Technologies (Vikrant Goyal). — [LiveLaw Fujitsu](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/delhi-consumer-court-directs-fujitsu-general-to-refund-114-lakh-for-defective-ac-awards-25k-compensation-532250); [LiveLaw Lloyd](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/hisar-district-commission-holds-lloyd-electric-and-its-seller-liable-for-selling-defective-ac-and-failing-to-rectify-issues-244613); [LiveLaw LG](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/failure-to-repair-acs-within-warranty-jaipur-district-commission-holds-lg-its-dealer-liable-for-deficiency-in-service-251771); [Vikrant Goyal v. UrbanClap](https://livelaw.in/pdf_upload/case-307-vikrant-goyal-vs-urban-clap-technologies-india-pvt-ltd-and-another-512225.pdf)

### Inferences
- NCH volume is dominated by e-commerce; durables complaints are roughly a sixth of e-commerce volume. The 2023 dip versus 2022 and 2024 may reflect different measures (the source lists top-5 tables only).

### Gaps
- No current service-centre or engineer counts for LG, Whirlpool, Godrej, Voltas, Daikin, IFB or Haier. An LG figure (8,000 engineers, 1,200 centres) came from an old (circa 2008) article and should not be used.
- No appliance-specific NCH sub-category counts and no NCDRC-level statistics. I could not confirm an NCDRC 2026 ruling on appliance repair (a CDJ entry dated 19 Jun 2026 had no details).

## Installed base, repair spend, informal technicians, skilling

### Takeaway
Reliable figures are thin. The only India appliance-repair market estimate found is a single-firm number (about USD 1B). The service base is fragmented and technician demand is rising sharply.

### Cited Findings
- BlueWeave: India home appliance repair service market about USD 980.7M in 2023, 8.18% CAGR to about USD 1.49B by 2030 (single firm; scope not verified). — [BlueWeave](https://blueweaveconsulting.com/report/india-home-appliance-repair-service-market)
- CEEW, as cited by Credence Research: skilled AC technician demand rises from about 0.37M (2022-23) to about 2M by 2036-37. — [Credence](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/india-ac-servicing-market)
- Water-purifier service: unorganised local technicians hold about half the market (Market N Tel). — [MarkNtel](https://www.marknteladvisors.com/research-library/india-water-purifier-service-market-report.html)
- KPMG's Q1 FY27 consumer durables note says Skill India develops technicians to improve white-goods after-sales service. — [KPMG, Sep 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/in/pdf/2026/09/the-indian-consumer-durables-sector-q1fy27.pdf)
- Republic World reports a shift from informal technicians to organised networks (journalistic, no data). — [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/business/the-end-of-the-local-repair-shop-how-indias-appliance-ecosystem-is-changing)

### Inferences
- Cross-checking: Samsung's 12,500 engineers against a demand estimate of hundreds of thousands of technicians suggests OEM networks cover only a minority of repair labour.

### Gaps
- No installed-base count (units in use) found. No credible informal-technician share for all appliances. No NSDC or Skill India appliance-sector numbers beyond the above. Urban Company's training scale was not found.

### Takeaway
Market-size estimates vary widely and no penetration or attach rate was found.

### Cited Findings
- IMARC: India extended warranty market USD 4.40B in 2025, to USD 10.04B by 2034 (9.6% CAGR). — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-extended-warranty-market)

Allied Market Research gives USD 305B for 2021, which is implausible. — [Credence](https://www.credenceresearch.com/report/india-consumer-electronics-extended-warranty-market); [Allied](https://www.alliedmarketresearch.com/india-consumer-electronics-extended-warranty-market-A53726)
- Perceived high plan cost restricts penetration in a price-sensitive market (Credence). — same Credence link

### Inferences
- Treat the USD 3.6-4.4B estimates as the only usable range, and as upper-bound and unverified.

### Gaps
- No attach rate (share of durables sold with an extended warranty or AMC). Finaccord's paid report might hold it.
