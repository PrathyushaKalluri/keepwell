# Competitors, existing solutions and workarounds: AI-assisted job prep at small appliance repair agencies (Scenario A), India first (as of Nov 2026)

Note: about 11 tool calls used. Many vendor facts rest on search snippets, not full-page reads. Items marked UNVERIFIED need a primary-source check. Revin AI, ServiceTitan, ServicePower and Marcone/mSupply tools were not covered by any search (gaps below).

## 1. Direct and adjacent products: what exists and what each does not do

### Takeaway
Global FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro, Zoho FSM) cover scheduling, dispatch and quotes but not fault hypotheses or parts-to-carry. AI-diagnosis vendors (Bernard, Aquant, Bruviti, Fieldproxy agent) are US/enterprise-oriented and sell to OEMs or US shops. I found no India-first, vernacular, voice/WhatsApp-first tool that covers the intake-to-job-card-to-parts chain for small agencies. Absence of evidence in about 11 searches, not proof.

### Cited Findings
**Generic FSM**
- Zoho FSM: Zoho's help page describes appointment-volume-based pricing (Standard base listed at $30/month, USD). Indian partner sites quote ₹1,000 (Standard) and ₹1,800 (Professional) per user per month ex-GST, with a free tier for 2 users. The two pricing models conflict, so verify with Zoho. — [Zoho help](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/fsm/getting-started/articles/fsm-pricing); [PrecisionTech partner](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-fsm/zoho-fsm-in-gudgaon/)
- Jobber entry is about $39/month per Software Advice (updated 2 Nov 2026). Another aggregator says $49/user Core. Housecall Pro entry estimates are $59-79/month, and some say it does not publish pricing. Aggregators conflict and most are affiliate sites, so treat as UNVERIFIED. — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/construction/housecall-profile/vs/jobber); [CostBench](https://costbench.com/compare/housecall-pro-vs-jobber/)
- None of the comparison results mentioned appliance-repair-specific features, and they are US/Canada-oriented. Language support and India fit were not stated (UNVERIFIED). — same sources.
- Not searched: ServiceTitan, ServicePower (gap).

**Fieldproxy (appliance diagnostic agent)**
- Voice agent that captures symptom, identifies likely cause, offers DIY steps or books a visit. It searches the business's manuals and service history and returns cited answers. Targets appliance repair field-service teams. The page states no languages, pricing or geography. Fieldproxy also hosts FSM pricing comparison pages. Funding and traction not found. — [Fieldproxy agent page](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/ai-agents/appliance-diagnostic-agent); [Fieldproxy HP pricing page](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/fsm-software-pricing/housecall-pro)
- It appears customer-facing (phone triage), not technician job card or parts-to-carry (inference from page; UNVERIFIED).

**Bernard (US)**
- Positioned as an AI operating system for appliance trades. Pre-ID combines appliance, symptoms, repair history, similar visits, manufacturer guidance and parts data to output a diagnosis, ranked parts and follow-up questions. Also 24/7 call handling that captures symptoms and checks warranty. Describes itself as building high-performing appliance servicers (possibly an operator/roll-up model, UNVERIFIED). Pricing, funding and India presence not found in results. — [Extruct profile](https://www.extruct.ai/hub/bernardhq-com/); [F4 fund page](https://f4.fund/startups/bernardhq)
- Closest functional match to Scenario A (fault hypotheses plus ranked parts). English/US focus is inferred, not stated.

Enterprise/OEM target. — [Field Service News](https://www.fieldservicenews.com/aquant-announces-70-million-series-c-funding-round-to-revolutionize-the-service-industry-using-artificial-intelligence/); [Aquant Service Hero](https://www.aquant.ai/service-hero/)

## 2. Current workarounds agencies use

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and mostly indirect. The strongest sourced pattern is a parts-access and training gap for independent technicians, who rely on salvaged parts, local dealers and online tutorials. WhatsApp groups, paper slips, notebooks, spreadsheets and brand IVR are plausible but not evidenced in my results.

### Cited Findings
- Independent technicians struggle with genuine parts access and training; OEMs favour their own service centres; technicians frequently salvage parts from dead devices. — [Toxics Link/TheNewsMinute Hyderabad study](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/telangana/why-hyderabads-it-household-repair-shops-have-become-bad-at-fixing-your-devices-study); [Toxics Link report](https://toxicslink.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Electronic%20Repair%20report.pdf)
- Report recommends mandating OEM access to manuals and genuine parts for independent technicians. — same sources.
- YouTube is cited as a learning resource, and buying from authorized dealers or local suppliers with testing of parts is advised, in a small-business guide (low-authority blog). — [money-make guide](https://money-make.pages.dev/blog/repair-services)
- Authorized service is promoted by brands over local repair. — [LG India guide](https://www.lg.com/in/magazine/ac-installation-service-repair-guide/)
- NSDC has an official qualification pack for the trade (formal skilling exists, adoption unknown). — [Electronics Media](https://www.electronicsmedia.info/?p=82098)
- WhatsApp use: only found a WhatsApp-based job-search platform (Bino), not agency workflow evidence. — [Bino](https://bino.bot/find/appliance-repair-technician-jobs-ac-fridge-washing-machine-bangalore)
- UNVERIFIED (no source): paper call slips, notebooks, phone calls, Telegram/WhatsApp parts groups, brand IVR, spreadsheets. Need primary interviews.

### Inferences
- The parts-access gap suggests parts-to-carry prediction has real value, because a wasted trip is costly when parts come from local dealers.
- Workarounds are free and familiar (WhatsApp, YouTube), so willingness to pay is the main risk (inference).

### Gaps
- No direct evidence on dispatch practices, call-slip handling or repeat-failure tracking. Recommend 8-10 agency owner interviews.

## 3. Gaps and defensibility

### Takeaway
Gap is a vernacular, voice/WhatsApp-first, small-agency-priced desk that links intake to job card to parts. Incumbent response: FSM vendors and Fieldproxy can add LLM intake quickly; hard-to-copy assets are local-language messy-speech handling, regional parts and dealer data, and agency-specific repeat-failure history.

### Cited Findings
- Zoho FSM already has Indian distribution and per-user INR pricing via partners, a price anchor of roughly ₹1,000-1,800/user/month. — [PrecisionTech](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-fsm/zoho-fsm-in-gudgaon/)
- WhatsApp Business API costs in India are layered: Meta per-message fees plus BSP fee. Rates vary by source (utility about ₹0.115, marketing ₹0.78-1.09; sources conflict, UNVERIFIED). — [Whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india); [Heltar](https://www.heltar.com/blogs/whatsapp-api-pricing-in-different-countries-cm2tidr3d00cdwixo7c0yned6)
- Fieldproxy's agent already cites manuals and service history in answers, so retrieval-grounded diagnosis is replicable. — [Fieldproxy](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/ai-agents/appliance-diagnostic-agent)

### Inferences
- Gemini-based desk can plausibly do: Hindi/Hinglish/regional voice intake from noisy customer calls, photo/video of fault and model plate, brand-and-model-specific hypotheses, job card in the technician's language on WhatsApp, parts-to-carry list, repeat-failure flags across the agency's history.
- Incumbents cannot easily copy: Indian-language field data, local dealer/parts availability and pricing, trust with unorganised agencies, low-ticket pricing.
- Incumbents can copy easily: generic LLM call summarization, manual RAG, quote templates, WhatsApp notifications.
- Risks: OEM-side AI diagnostics (Samsung) deepening, horizontal Indian voice agents verticalizing, Urban Company-style marketplaces locking supply.

### Gaps
- No evidence on accuracy of any vendor's diagnoses, customer traction numbers for Fieldproxy or Bernard, or small-agency willingness to pay in India.
