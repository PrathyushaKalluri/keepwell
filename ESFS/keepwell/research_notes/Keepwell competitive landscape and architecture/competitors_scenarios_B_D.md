# Competitors, workarounds and retention: appliance records, warranty tracking, AMC renewal, consumer claim help (Scenarios B and D)

Research budget: 8 searches, no page fetches. Search tool returned summarised results, so figures are from snippets and are flagged where secondary. Date: Nov 2026.

## 1. Warranty / home-record apps and administrators: who exists, what they do

### Takeaway
The India market is dominated by B2B2C warranty administrators (OneAssist, Servify, Onsitego, Zopper) that sell plans via retailers/OEMs; consumer-first brand-agnostic "warranty wallet" apps in India are tiny (about 1,000 downloads at TOTOKO as of 2022). Abroad, home-record apps (Centriq, HomeBinder) pivoted to B2B (insurers, realtors, HVAC/home-services) or faded. No evidence found of any consumer-first record app with traction in India.

### Cited Findings
- OneAssist FY25 operating revenue Rs 623 cr (up 22% from Rs 509 cr), net loss about Rs 11 cr; smartphones/personal electronics about 60% of revenue — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-peak-xv-backed-oneassist-to-raise-rs-108-cr-from-stride-ventures-10064749); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-oneassists-ai-stack-is-giving-older-smartphones-an-insured-future/) (figures as relayed by search summary; verify)
- OneAssist claims about 2 crore customers across 19,000 pin codes — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/oneassist-consumer-solutions) (company claim via aggregator)
- Servify FY24 revenue about Rs 755 cr, net loss about Rs 94 cr, India 56.8% / US 38.6% of revenue, valuation about $680-700M — [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/startup-deep-dive/servify-deep-dive/) (secondary; unverified against filings)
- Servify began (2016, BeenNext-backed) as a consumer "digital assistant" that stores bills and warranties on the cloud and links to authorised service; reported over half a million devices managed shortly after launch — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/servify-now-a-digital-assistant-for-your-devices-116120500054_1.html); [e27](https://e27.co/beenext-backs-servify-to-help-you-keep-a-track-of-all-your-electronic-devices-appliances-20161124/). Revenue today is overwhelmingly B2B (OEM/telco service and plans), i.e. the consumer wallet was the entry, not the business (inference).
- Onsitego: Series B extended to $30M with IFC — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/onsitego-raises-capital-from-ifc-extends-series-b-round-to-30-mn); no current revenue found.
- Zopper markets extended-warranty solutions for OEMs and retailers; cites India consumer-electronics extended warranty market USD 3.58B (2023) to 8.80B (2032) — [Zopper blog](https://www.zopper.com/blog/extended-warranty-solutions-for-oems-and-retailers-in-india) (vendor marketing; treat cautiously)
- Small India consumer apps: TOTOKO (Delhi, launched Sept 2021; over 1,000 downloads and one-touch customer care for 1,000+ brands by May 2022) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2022/05/delhi-based-startup-helping-households-msmes-manage-bills-and-warranty-totoko); CheckMyWarranty (documents/reminders app) — [eChai](https://echai.ventures/brands/checkmywarranty); eWarranty Solutions (Pune, OEM/service-provider focused) — [Startuppedia](https://startuppedia.in/startup-stories/meet-the-man-who-once-ironed-clothes-now-launches-a-startup-with-friend-that-digitizes-the-warranty-process-8742483); Product Hunt-type entrants (Warranti launched May 2025, HoldMyBill, SnapHome Feb 2026) — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/warranti/launches), [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.nchitimilla.snaphome). No traction data for these.

Search found no shutdown notice for Centriq/HomeBinder; current status unverified.
- How data enters: invoice/photo upload and manual entry, barcode/model scan (Centriq), OEM account registration (LG: model, serial, purchase date at lg.com/in — third-party guide), retailer-sold plans where the seller already holds the invoice (administrators). - OEM apps are unreliable as record of truth: SamMobile (Oct 2026) reports Galaxy buyers in India seeing shorter warranty in Samsung Members; Samsung says warranty runs from invoice date — [SamMobile](https://sammobile.com/news/samsung-faces-complaints-galaxy-phones-showing-shorter-warranty-periods-india). - Warranty start-date ambiguity (order vs delivery vs installation) was taken up by the consumer regulator CCPA with appliance makers, June 2024 — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/ccpa-appliance-makers-discuss-issue-of-commencement-of-warranty-period/2143793); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/government-asks-electronics-firms-to-clearly-alert-consumers-about-warranty-follow-global-practices-2024-06-22-938256)

### What they do NOT do (inferences from the above)
- OEM apps: single-brand only, may hold wrong dates, no cross-brand view. Administrators: only cover plans they sold; the customer relationship is owned by the retailer/OEM. Wallet apps: depend on manual entry; no claim execution. None found that links invoice + manual + service history + plan expiry + complaint drafting.

### Gaps
- Samsung Members, LG ThinQ, Whirlpool, Godrej app features/ratings/installs: not researched (search returned only warranty policy pages). Amazon/Flipkart order-history as record: no sourced evidence found (invoice download exists, but not verified here). Pricing/MAU for Centriq, HomeBinder, Sortly: not found. Onsitego and Zopper current revenue: not found.

## 2. AMC / service plans in India: renewal practices and price points

### Takeaway
Plans are sold at point of sale or online by retailer-administrators; public pricing is mostly for AC servicing. Coverage is often narrow, and plan terms limit the use of customer data to the plan.

### Cited Findings
- Croma ZipCare AC AMC: 1-year Rs 1,999 (list Rs 2,499) and 2-year Rs 3,599 (list Rs 4,499), 20% auto-discount in cart; covers wet servicing and gas charging, not repair labour/parts; plans non-transferable; total coverage across plans capped at 5 years — [Croma 1Y AMC](https://croma.com/zipcare-maintain-amc-plan-for-air-conditioner-1-year/p/272182), [Croma ZipCare](https://croma.com/zipcare). Renewal terms not found.
- Reliance resQ Care Plan is an extended warranty (not AMC), only for Reliance Retail purchases, activates after brand warranty expiry, covers compressor, fan motor, PCB, remote; excludes drain pipe, stabiliser, stand, filters — [Reliance Digital](https://www.reliancedigital.in/product/4-year-resq-care-plan-rcp-extended-warranty-air-conditioner-from-rs-80000-to-rs-1l-9500851). Price not found.
- Samsung Care+ / Care Pack sold via Samsung and Flipkart with T&Cs — [Samsung TnC PDF](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/assets/in/offer/samsung-care-plus/appliances/Samsung-Care-Plus-Extended-Warranty-TnC-v1.pdf), [Flipkart](https://www.flipkart.com/pages/samsung-ext-warranty-tnc).
- OneAssist T&Cs state it will not contact customers other than for services under the plan term; grievance contact 18001233330 — [Flipkart VAS T&C](https://www.flipkart.com/pages/vas-new-tnc). Whether this constrains renewal outreach is unclear (unverified).

### Inferences
- Renewal triggers likely: end of OEM warranty (extended warranty offered at sale or just before expiry), seasonal pre-summer AC servicing offers (Croma "ZipCare Summer Offers" page exists: [Croma](https://www.croma.com/lp-zipcare-offers)). Direct evidence of renewal reminder cadence/channels was not found.

### Gaps
- Renewal triggers, channels (SMS/WhatsApp/call), renewal rates, AMC prices for Urban Company, brand service centres, local AMC vendors: not found.

## 3. Claim / complaint help

### Takeaway
Government channels are high-volume and growing; WhatsApp is now 30% of NCH intake. Private legal-tech for consumer claims in India was not evidenced by search; abroad, DoNotPay is a cautionary tale on overclaiming.

### Cited Findings
- NCH volume: 12.36 lakh complaints FY2023-24, 13.16 lakh FY2024-25, 20.49 lakh FY2025-26 per a Lok Sabha reply as reported — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/541-lakh-complaints-received-by-national-consumer-helpline-23-complaints-from-southern-states-says-nidhi-khare-secretary-of-the-department-of-consumer-affairs) (the exact article-to-figure mapping is unverified; periods not strictly comparable); another report: 14.2 lakh grievances received and over 13 lakh disposed in 2025 — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/nch-website-receives-over-142-lakh-grievances-disposes-of-more-than-13-lakh-in-2025-govt)
- Refund-related: 67,265 complaints and about Rs 45 cr refunds facilitated Apr-Dec 2025; e-commerce about 40,000 complaints and Rs 32 cr — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/national-consumer-helpline-ends-2025-with-rs-45-crore-recovered-for-citizens), [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/consumer-affairs-dept-facilitates-rs-45-cr-refunds-in-8-months-as-e-commerce-tops-complaints-86672.htm)
- WhatsApp share of NCH complaints about 30% — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/national-consumer-helpline-receives-one-lakh-complaints-per-month-hike-in-grievance-registration-through-whatsapp)
- e-Jagriti (successor to e-Daakhil) 2025: 1,62,474 cases filed, 1,50,197 disposed — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/1-50-lakh-consumer-grievances-out-of-1-62-lakh-cases-disposed-of-via-e-jagriti-portal-in-2025-mos/) (another report gives 1,30,300 filed to Oct 2025: conflicting window). No e-Daakhil-specific stats found.
- Need: LocalCircles survey (28,000+ respondents) found about 46% of Indian consumers stuck with high-value products with inherent defects; later survey 53% did not get refund — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/nearly-50-percentage-consumers-stuck-with-inherently-defective-highvalue-products-due-to-broken-redressal-system-localcircles/66777.html), [Business Standard 2024](https://business-standard.com/industry/news/53-consumers-did-not-get-refund-for-inherently-defective-products-survey-124031500916_1.html); about 1 in 2 households cannot repair white goods due to cost — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/why-india-needs-right-to-repair-1-in-2-households-unable-to-repair-whitegoods-and-appliances-due-to-prohibitive-cost/67798.html)
- Complaint-site evidence of plan-seller friction: OneAssist reviews alleging claim rejection/mis-selling — [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.oneassist.in); Onsitego complaints on repair delay and forced sale at retail — [Voxya](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/repair-delays-for-a-month/74590). Self-selected, unverified.
- DoNotPay: FTC finalised order Feb 2025, $193,000 monetary relief, barred from claiming to substitute for professionals without evidence — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/87474), [Law.com](https://law.com/legaltechnews/2024/09/25/ftc-fines-donotpay-193000-alleging-false-claims-over-robot-lawyer-service)
- Legal position: lost paper invoice should not void warranty; consumer courts accept other proof of purchase — [Jurigram blog](https://jurigram.com/blog/consumer-law/warranty-denied-because-invoice-lost-consumer-rights) (law-firm blog, commentary)

### Inferences
- Design implication: a complaint draft must be framed as assistance with evidence (invoice, dates, service tickets), not legal advice, given the DoNotPay precedent. A well-assembled evidence pack is the missing input to NCH/e-Jagriti filings.

### Gaps
- LegalKart and other Indian consumer-complaint services (features, pricing, traction): search returned nothing. Complaint resolution times for appliance claims: not found.

## 5. Why record apps fail to retain, and what makes a record sticky

### Takeaway
Data-entry burden and low frequency of need are the recurring explanations; evidence is anecdotal, not systematic.

### Cited Findings
- Entry friction determines survival; rule of thumb that over 30 seconds per item causes drop-off (vendor blog, not measured) — [Manifest blog](https://manifestwithme.pages.dev/blog/best-home-inventory-apps)
- Product Hunt thread: people stop updating after the first week, forget, other household members do not participate, benefit not worth effort — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/expirymate-food-tracker/what-makes-you-abandon-a-food-tracking-app) (food-tracking app; analogous)
- Reviewers prefer barcode scan; guides suggest photo-first, details later — [Bob Vila](https://www.bobvila.com/articles/home-inventory-apps/), [MacRumors thread](https://spy.macrumors.com/threads/not-impressed-with-the-home-inventory.2098481)
- Survivors found were those where a B2B payer exists: Centriq/HomeServe, Sheltr/Hippo, Servify B2B revenue (see section 1).

### Inferences
- Sticky record would: ingest automatically (forwarded e-invoices, Amazon/Flipkart invoice emails, WhatsApp share of photos) so zero entry; be rewarded at moments of need (breakdown, warranty expiry, plan renewal, complaint) rather than via a "maintain your inventory" habit; be shared across household members; and produce an output (claim-ready evidence pack) that the OEM apps cannot. All hypotheses, not tested.

### Gaps
- No quantified retention/churn data for any warranty or home-inventory app; no post-mortems found for Indian wallets (TOTOKO status after 2022 unknown).
