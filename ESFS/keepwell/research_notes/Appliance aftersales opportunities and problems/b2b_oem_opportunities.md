# B2B and OEM-facing opportunities and problems in appliance after-sales (global, India emphasis; as of Nov 2026)

Method note: research used WebSearch (summarised results) plus two page fetches, about 15 tool calls. Many web-search summaries did not expose page-level text, so several items are marked "unverified" or sit in Gaps. Dates in some search summaries looked inconsistent (e.g. pages dated 2026 with undated snippets); treat those as needing a check against the primary source. Vendor/consultancy/marketing claims are labelled.

## OEM problems (warranty cost, call-centre, NPS, parts forecasting, EU R2R, India EPR/refurb, returns, third-party service)

### Takeaway
Regulation is turning repair from a cost centre into a compliance obligation: EU Right to Repair (transposition deadline 31 July 2026, patchy compliance) and India's EPR price band and refurbishing certificates both create new data, parts and reporting duties for OEMs. Hard, current numbers on OEM warranty cost, call-centre load and parts forecasting were NOT found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- EU Right to Repair Directive 2024/1799 entered into force 30 July 2024; Member States had to transpose by 31 July 2026; further notification duties run to 31 July 2029 and a Commission report is due by 31 July 2031 — [Search summary citing Bird & Bird / Cleary Gottlieb / repair.eu](https://twobirds.com/en/insights/2024/germany/recht-auf-reparatur-richtlinie-veroeffentlicht-auswirkungen-fuer-wirtschaftsakteure-in-der-eu); [repair.eu](https://repair.eu/news/the-right-to-repair-directive/)
- Transposition is patchy: one article (secondary, undated in results) reports Commission letters of formal notice on 25 Sep 2026 to 18 states that had not notified complete transposition, and that as of 22 Oct only Germany and Italy among major markets had transposed. Conflicting: the same article says Italy completed on 7 Oct, so its list may be stale. Unverified — [Pasquale Pillitteri country tracker](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/26277/right-to-repair-europe-country-by-country)
- Germany: Ministry of Justice published a draft bill on 15 Jan 2026 (core rules in the BGB); an IHK Lippe report says the act has been in force since 23 July (no primary confirmation found) — [Cleary Gottlieb on German draft bill](https://www.clearygottlieb.com/news-and-insights/publication-listing/german-draft-bill-implementing-directive-eu-2024-1799); [Buse](https://buse.de/en/blog-en/commercial-en/right-to-repair-now-enshrined-in-german-law/)
- Cleary Gottlieb's manufacturer memo exists but the PDF was not machine-readable here, so spare-parts pricing rules were not confirmed — [Cleary memo PDF](https://www.clearygottlieb.com/-/media/files/alert-memos-2026/germanys-new-right-to-repair-what-manufacturers-must-know.pdf)
- ESPR first working plan adopted 16 April 2025 for 2025-2030, revisited after three years; carries over ~14 energy-related products from the 2022-24 plan. Dishwashers/washing machines are listed by secondary sources for 2026-27 but dates conflict; household tumble dryers 2027 is unconfirmed — [Commission working plan page](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/policy-making-0_da); [Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/ja/insights/newsupdates/2025/04/european-commission-releases-list-of-priority-products); [One Click LCA (vendor)](https://oneclicklca.com/en-gb/resources/articles/first-espr-working-plan)
- India E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022 in force 1 Apr 2023; 106 equipment categories in Schedule I; recycling targets 60% (FY24, FY25), 70% (FY26, FY27), 80% from FY28 — [GreenSutra explainer (consultancy/vendor)](https://greensutra.in/news/e-waste-epr-explained-2026/)
- 2024 amendment (effective 8 Mar 2024) lets CPCB set EPR certificate exchange prices at 30%-100% of environmental compensation, and authorises digital trading platforms; CPCB issued trading-platform guidelines Jan 2025 and final environmental-compensation guidelines 25 Aug 2025 — [GreenSutra](https://greensutra.in/news/e-waste-epr-explained-2026/); [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/e-waste-management-amendment-rules-2024-notified-includes-provisions-for-relaxing-the-period-for-filing-annual-and-quarterly-returns/); [TNPCB gazette copy](https://tnpcb.gov.in/owems/pdf/regulations/EwasteRules2022_AmendMar24.pdf)
- Price band challenged by large manufacturers in Delhi High Court since late 2024; an interim order in Jan 2026 stayed enforcement against one petitioner; no final judgment as of the June 2026 page update — [GreenSutra](https://greensutra.in/news/e-waste-epr-explained-2026/). Rupee floor figures conflict across sources (about Rs 22/kg floor cited by one; Rs 10-50/kg range by another) — [Chatter (Zerodha)](https://thechatter.zerodha.com/p/how-indias-e-waste-rules-found-their); treat as unverified.
- Refurbishing: registered refurbishers issue certificates that defer a producer's EPR obligation by the extended product life (example: 5-year extension counts 60 tonnes in year one) — [GreenSutra](https://greensutra.in/news/e-waste-epr-explained-2026/); [search summary of rules](https://www.mondaq.com/india/waste-management/1374872/the-e-waste-management-rules-2022)
- Parts/spares relief for equipment placed on market on or before 1 Apr 2025 runs to 1 Apr 2028 where RoHS-compliant parts are unavailable — [GreenSutra](https://greensutra.in/news/e-waste-epr-explained-2026/)
- Warranty cost benchmarks are old: Warranty Week put appliance makers at 3%+ of revenue in 2008 (Whirlpool ~2.8% in 2005) and US appliance makers ex-GE at a little under 1.3% of product revenue in 2015; a 2020 Whirlpool spike was traced to Indesit-built horizontal-axis washers — [Warranty Week 2008](https://www.warrantyweek.com/archive/ww20080925.html); [Warranty Week 2015](https://www.warrantyweek.com/archive/ww20150604.html); [Warranty Week 2020](https://warrantyweek.com/archive/mobile/mobile_20200604.html)
- Service-parts/after-sales AI software for OEMs: Bruviti raised $6M (total $11M) in Aug 2025 — [Bruviti resources](https://bruviti.com/?p=2953) (via search summary; unverified amount/date detail)

### Inferences
- A structural OEM pain is data: every regime (R2R information form, ESPR/DPP, EPR sales-and-returns filings) needs per-SKU parts, repairability and sales data that OEM ERPs usually lack. This is an inference, not directly sourced.
- India's price band and the Delhi HC dispute mean EPR cost is uncertain for OEMs, which favours tools for price hedging and certificate sourcing.
- Third-party service enablement is plausible as OEMs must make parts and repair info available to independent repairers under R2R (inference; pricing rules unconfirmed).

### Gaps
- Current OEM warranty cost % (2023-26), call-centre load, NPS data: not found; need annual-report notes or Warranty Week recent issues.
- R2R spare-parts pricing text ("reasonable price") and the EU repair-information form specifics: not confirmed; check EUR-Lex 2024/1799.
- Parts forecasting problem evidence: no sourced material.
- Consultancy (Deloitte/PwC/BCG/McKinsey) briefings were not retrieved.

## B2B2C AMC (housing societies, PG/co-living, hotels, hospitals, commercial kitchens) and solar/EV-adjacent machines

### Takeaway
Solar inverters/batteries and EV chargers show documented after-sales gaps and are plausible adjacent markets; housing-society/PG/hotel/commercial-kitchen AMC evidence was NOT found in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Rooftop solar O&M in India is a disorganised market with thin economics and customer resistance to long-term contracts — [Bridge to India](https://bridgetoindia.com/rooftop-solar-om-needs-urgent-policy-intervention/)
- CEEW residential study: households are hesitant to renew paid maintenance after the first five years; owners report inverter-service delays and unclear warranty responsibility — [CEEW](https://www.ceew.in/node/4977); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1035720/solar-panels-are-spreading-across-india-but-who-is-keeping-them-working)
- Public EV charger reliability is a recognised problem; IEEFA recommends uptime requirements; Bolt.Earth (vendor) cites 71% of public stations operational; a 55-60% uptime figure from EVTech is unsourced and conflicts with a 90-94% industry average cited by Bolt.Earth — [IEEFA](https://www.evinfrastructurenews.com/ev-regulations/india-needs-more-reliable-affordable-ev-charging-to-boost-adoption-ieefa); [Bolt.Earth](https://bolt.earth/blog/indias-ev-charger-gap-only-71-public-ev-charging-stations-are-operational); [EVTech](https://evtech.news/news/india-s-ev-charging-stations-are-growing-fast-but-72-still-face-reliability-issues.html)
- EV service centres limited; most providers lack diagnostic tools — [EVreporter](https://evreporter.com/ev-service-in-india-challenges-and-emerging-opportunities/)
- Early Indian appliance-service startups: Wiffy $3M Series A led by Earth Fund (date unconfirmed, perhaps 2026); Eazzy ~$440K angel round announced 28 May 2026 (AC/appliance repair, Gurugram) — [Ascendants on Wiffy](https://ascendants.in/business-stories/wiffy-raises-3-million-series-a-earth-fund/); [Raising.fi on Eazzy](https://raising.fi/news/eazzy-angel-may-2026)

### Inferences
- Adjacent-market answer: yes for solar inverters/batteries and EV chargers on need (documented service gaps), but renewal reluctance and thin O&M economics limit AMC models; B2B contracts (installers, CPOs, OEM channel partners) look better than direct-to-household (inference).

### Gaps
- Housing society AMC, PG/co-living, hotel, hospital non-critical, commercial kitchen, cafe/clinic/salon demand and pricing: no sourced data found.
- Heat pump and solar water heater service: not searched productively.
