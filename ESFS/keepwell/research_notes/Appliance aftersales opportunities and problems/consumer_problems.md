# Consumer-side problems and unmet needs in household appliance after-sales (India first, then global), as of Nov 2026

Evidence labels: [MEASURED] = survey/official data with stated sample or counts; [SELF-SELECTED] = LocalCircles-style online polls (large n but not representative); [ANECDOTAL] = individual complaints/news cases; [VENDOR-EST] = market-research firm estimate; [UNVERIFIED] = reported only via search-result summary, original not opened. Note: most findings below came from search-result summaries; only the ThePrint page was opened in full (a MoneyLife fetch returned 403). Treat figures as needing a second check before publication. Research budget was limited (about 16 calls); coverage of Reddit/Quora/Play Store, elderly/language, and rural segments is thin and flagged in Gaps.

## 1. What do Indian consumers complain about most, and how big is it? (complaint volumes, warranty, defects)

### Takeaway
Consumer durables is a top-5 sector on the National Consumer Helpline (NCH), at roughly 39k to 78k grievances a year, behind e-commerce. LocalCircles polls (self-selected) suggest about half of households own an inherently defective high-value product, and few get a refund or replacement. Consumer courts routinely rule against brands on warranty refusal, spare-part shortages and repair delays, but awards are small (about Rs 5k-25k), which limits deterrence.

### Cited Findings
- NCH sector data (Rajya Sabha annexure, via search summary [UNVERIFIED, MEASURED]): Consumer Durables had 49,066 disposed grievances in 2023 (rank 5), 77,873 in 2024 (rank 2), and 38,885 in 2025 (rank 4); e-commerce was 440,185 in 2024 and 365,370 in 2025. The summary itself flagged this as needing confirmation against the primary annexure. — [Rajya Sabha PQ 267 of 18-03-2025](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/759128/1/PQ_267_18032025_U1951_p192_p193.pdf); coverage: [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/5-1-lakh-complaints-filed-against-ecommerce-platforms-in-2025-govt/)
- A recent Lok Sabha reply lists consumer durables and electronic products among leading NCH complaint sectors after e-commerce (5,77,748 e-commerce grievances in 2025-26); NCH facilitated over Rs 91 crore in refunds from 25 Apr 2025 to 30 Jun 2026, of which over Rs 63 crore e-commerce [UNVERIFIED, from search summary] — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/flipkart-amazon-among-top-e-commerce-platforms-with-highest-consumer-complaints-in-2025-govt); [AIR/newsonair](https://newsonair.gov.in/toll-free-number-in-17-languages-for-consumers-grievance-redressal-across-the-country-govt-in-ls/)
- NCH website received 14.2 lakh grievances in 2025 and disposed over 13 lakh (all sectors) — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/nch-website-receives-more-than-142-lakh-grievances-disposes-over-13-lakh-in-2025-govt--20251202164259)
- [SELF-SELECTED] LocalCircles March 2024, 32,000+ responses from 311 districts: at least 55% of households had at least one high-value product found inherently defective in warranty; of those who sought action, only 21% got a replacement/refund; more than half got neither. Earlier 2022 poll: 46% stuck with a defective product; 3 in 10 got no help from the brand. — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/industry/news/53-consumers-did-not-get-refund-for-inherently-defective-products-survey-124031500916_1.html); [LocalCircles press page](https://www.localcircles.com/a/press/page/consumer-survey-on-defective-products); [MoneyLife](https://moneylife.in/article/nearly-50-percentage-consumers-stuck-with-inherently-defective-highstakes-products-due-to-broken-redressal-system-localcircles/66777.html) (URL as returned by search: https://moneylife.in/article/nearly-50-percentage-consumers-stuck-with-inherently-defective-highvalue-products-due-to-broken-redressal-system-localcircles/66777.html)
- [SELF-SELECTED] 94% of respondents want CCPA to take suo motu action against brands selling defective products (same 2022 poll) — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/one-in-two-consumers-stuck-with-defective-gadgets-survey-shows-122033001629_1.html)
- [SELF-SELECTED] 2024 LocalCircles poll of 36,000+ respondents: most households repaired TVs after warranty expiry; only 3% used in-warranty repair — [Business Standard summary via search](https://business-standard.com/industry/news/53-consumers-did-not-get-refund-for-inherently-defective-products-survey-124031500916_1.html) [UNVERIFIED which article carries this TV figure; see also [NewsMeter smart TV story](https://newsmeter.in/data-stories/smart-tvs-are-no-longer-smart-india-32-consumers-complained-problems-735124), which reports 32% complained of smart TV problems]
- Case law pattern [ANECDOTAL, case-by-case, from LiveLaw reports]: Ernakulam commission held Philips and dealer liable for refusing LED TV warranty, with repair or 50% refund — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/ernakulam-district-commission-holds-philips-india-and-bismi-appliances-liable-for-deficiency-in-service-over-refusal-of-led-tv-warranty-304683); Kerala commission rejected Godrej's "warranty subject to spare-parts availability" defence on an AC (Dec 2025) — [SupremeToday](https://supremetoday.ai/kerala-forum-slams-godrej-over-ac-warranty-failure-awards-compensation-2025121513171614e142); Delhi State Commission held Voltas liable for charging repair costs within warranty — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/delhi-state-commission-holds-voltas-liable-for-charging-repair-costs-for-ac-within-warranty-period-275033); Delhi court ordered Fujitsu General to refund Rs 1.14 lakh for a defective AC plus Rs 25k — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/delhi-consumer-court-directs-fujitsu-general-to-refund-114-lakh-for-defective-ac-awards-25k-compensation-532250); Mumbai commission held Reliance Retail liable for failing to replace a defective AC — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/mumbai-district-commission-holds-reliance-retail-liable-for-failure-to-replace-defective-air-conditioner-301308); Mumbai forum said TV maker, not third-party seller, owes compensation — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/mumbai-tv-makers-responsible-for-compensation-not-third-party-seller-consumer-forum-tells-manufacturer)
- Seller/manufacturer joint liability under Consumer Protection Act 2019 is the legal baseline — [Business Standard column](https://www.business-standard.com/opinion/columns/consumer-protection-seller-equally-liable-for-any-defective-product-125091400814_1.html)

### Inferences
- The recurring fact patterns (warranty refused, service centre unauthorised, spares unavailable, repair delay) are the "rejection tricks" consumers hit; the court route works but compensation (about Rs 5-25k) is low relative to time cost, so most consumers likely do not litigate. A pre-litigation evidence/claim tool (case-file generator, e-Daakhil filing helper, notice drafting) is a plausible product, but willingness to pay is unproven.
- NCH consumer-durables counts fell between 2024 and 2025 in the annexure; I cannot say whether this reflects fewer problems or category reclassification.

### Gaps
- Did not open the primary Rajya Sabha/Lok Sabha annexures, so sub-category (AC vs fridge vs TV) breakdown is missing.
- No NCH data on repair-specific vs warranty-specific vs spare-part sub-complaints.
- No Reddit (r/india, r/Appliances, r/appliancerepair), Quora, MouthShut, Play Store review mining; those searches were not run. Only Trustpilot/complaint-site snippets for Urban Company (see section 2).

## 2. Overcharging, price variance, misdiagnosis, repair-vs-replace

### Takeaway
Brand service is perceived as expensive (two-thirds say 50%+ above local vendors), so most AC owners avoid it, yet the informal alternative has no quality or price assurance. Platform-based repair (Urban Company) draws anecdotal complaints of upsell (gas refill), damage-then-charge, and warranty disputes.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-SELECTED] LocalCircles (June 2022, about 22,000 respondents; 20,891 answered the cost question): 76% of AC owners do not use manufacturer after-sales service; reasons: 65% high cost, 36% difficult contact process, 32% time-consuming to reach company; about two in three said the brand's service arm charged 50% or more than local vendors, 23% said over 100% more. — [ThePrint (opened in full)](https://theprint.in/india/76-indians-dont-get-acs-fixed-by-manufacturers-due-to-high-costs-bad-service-survey-shows/997052)
- [SELF-SELECTED] LocalCircles Jan 2022: 43% of participating households had three or more devices under five years old needing service/repair; broken after-sales economics and warranty processes were the main reason for buying new — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/article/economy-policy/localcircles-asks-moca-for-standard-after-sales-service-for-white-goods-122061501128_1.html); [MoneyLife headline "1 in 2 households unable to repair whitegoods due to prohibitive cost"](https://moneylife.in/article/why-india-needs-right-to-repair-1-in-2-households-unable-to-repair-whitegoods-and-appliances-due-to-prohibitive-cost/67798.html) [page not opened, 403]
- LocalCircles asked MoCA for standard after-sales rules for white goods and penalties for spare-part/service default in warranty — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/article/economy-policy/localcircles-asks-moca-for-standard-after-sales-service-for-white-goods-122061501128_1.html)
- [ANECDOTAL] Urban Company complaints on aggregator sites: forced gas refill, technician damage to AC then charge for repair, extra charges for diagnosed parts under "warranty" repair, add-on priced above MRP (Rs 253 vs MRP Rs 170 claimed), installation copper pipe charged Rs 250 extra per metre. — [Trustpilot](https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/www.urbancompany.com); [ConsumerComplaints.in (AC damaged)](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/urban-company-my-ac-is-damaged-by-urban-technician-c3014760); [Voxya May 2026](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/ac-service-technician-broke-my-ac/266530); [ConsumerComplaints.in (wrong assessment)](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/urban-company-wrong-assessment-of-ac-and-misleading-customer-c3544659); [ConsumerComplaints.in (90-day revisit warranty)](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/urban-company-split-ac-repair-revisit-90-days-warranty-even-i-claimed-for-the-same-issue-before-90-days-why-still-charging-us-c2868106); [blog](https://www.travellingcamera.com/2025/08/urban-company-where-diagnosis-is.html?m=0). These complaint sites are self-posted and unverified; they show types of grievance, not rate.
- Unorganised vendors reportedly serve over 80% of electronics repairs [VENDOR/MEDIA CLAIM, UNVERIFIED] — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/magazine/story/happily-ever-after-5330)
- Water-purifier research firm describes independent technicians using non-genuine parts at inconsistent rates [QUALITATIVE, VENDOR] — [MarkNtel](https://www.marknteladvisors.com/research-library/india-water-purifier-service-market-report.html)

### Inferences
- A large, repeatedly observed gap: price transparency and diagnostic verification (independent second opinion, fair-price benchmark for a given fault, photo evidence of replaced part). Evidence for it is anecdotal plus the LocalCircles cost-gap poll; no measured rate of fake parts or overcharging found.
- Repair-vs-replace decision aid (cost vs age vs energy efficiency) has no India-specific evidence found; Europe evidence in section 6 supports that economics drive replacement.

### Gaps
- No measured fake/non-genuine spare-part prevalence in India.
- No measured no-show rates, wait times (ThePrint article had none), or % of repairs failing/recurring.
- Urban Company's rate card exists but no price-variance dataset found.
- Play Store review mining of Urban Company, brand service apps (LG ThinQ, Samsung Members), Servify not done.

## 3. Safety incidents (gas geysers, electrical fires)

### Takeaway
Safety is a real but poorly measured problem: gas geyser carbon-monoxide deaths recur in news with no national statistic; electrical short circuits cause about 17% of fire deaths. No appliance-specific attribution exists in official data. This is a no-revenue/regulatory-trust area with possible B2B (landlord/housing society) angles.

### Cited Findings
- No national statistics on gas-geyser deaths exist and many are never linked to the heater (Tribune investigation) [MEASURED-ABSENCE] — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/in-depth/beware-of-gas-geysers)
- Bangalore cluster, more than a dozen bathroom CO deaths linked to LPG water heaters over about two years (2007) [ANECDOTAL/NEWS] — [India Environment Portal](https://www.indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/news-clippings/lpg-water-heaters-blamed-for-a-dozen-bangalore-deaths)
- Recent incidents: Mysuru two sisters (LPG geyser) — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/2-sisters-die-due-to-lpg-leakage-in-gas-geyser-in-mysuru); Bengaluru couple CO — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/bengaluru-couple-taking-shower-in-bathroom-dies-after-inhaling-carbon-monoxide-1227170.html); Hyderabad family of 3 — [NewsMeter](https://newsmeter.in/top-stories/hyderabad-tragedy-how-a-family-of-3-from-sanathnagar-died-after-inhaling-geyser-gas-732912); Puducherry three dead — [The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/puducherry-gas-leak-3-dead-two-hospitalised-after-inhaling-toxic-gas-in-washroom)
- CO poisoning is under-recognised and under-reported in India (Manipal academic abstract) — [Manipal](https://researcher.manipal.edu/en/publications/gas-geyser-a-cause-of-fatal-domestic-carbon-monoxide-poisoning/)
- NCRB via Dataful: 2024 had 5,888 fire accidental deaths; electrical short circuits 17.2%, cooking gas cylinder/stove bursts 23.6% — [Dataful](https://insights.dataful.in/articles/nearly-6000-fire-accident-deaths-in-2024-despite-sharp-decline-in-accidents). Short-circuit share grew from about 15% to about 21% of fires 2016-2020 — [FactChecker](https://factchecker.in/data-dive/data-dive-fire-accidents-steadily-dip-yet-manage-to-kill-35-people-a-day-in-5-years-816342)

### Inferences
- Opportunities: ventilation/CO-sensor advocacy, safety checks for rentals/hostels, aged-appliance wiring audit; revenue is thin (public good) but could ride on landlord compliance, insurance, or utility partnerships. Pure inference.

### Gaps
- No appliance-attributed fire statistics (fridge, AC, geyser, mixer); no BIS/CCPA recall or safety-notice data found.

## 4. Spare parts, right to repair policy, e-waste and disposal

### Takeaway
India has a Right to Repair portal and a proposed Repairability Index but no statutory right to repair; spare-part shortages show up in court cases. E-waste is rising and data on formal collection is inconsistent across sources.

### Cited Findings
- DoCA launched Right to Repair Portal India (Consumer Rights Day 2022), covering consumer durables among sectors; a Repairability Index framework for appliances was submitted (fridges and washing machines to be rated; spare-part availability a criterion); committee mostly industry reps; right to repair is not a statutory right in India — [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2012833); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/govt-launches-right-to-repair-portal-to-promote-circular-economy-reduce-e-waste/1993903/); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/repairability-index-1/print_manually); [IIMB note](https://www.iimb.ac.in/sites/default/files/2023-10/Right-Repair-India-step-right-direction.pdf). Current implementation status not verified [UNVERIFIED].
- Spare parts unavailability pleaded as a defence in warranty (Godrej case above) — [SupremeToday](https://supremetoday.ai/kerala-forum-slams-godrej-over-ac-warranty-failure-awards-compensation-2025121513171614e142)
- CPCB-based: e-waste 13.98 lakh tonnes in 2024-25 vs 12.54 lakh tonnes in 2023-24; share "collected/dismantled/recycled/disposed" 70.71% vs 61.94% (broader than formal recycling). Other sources cite 6.2 Mt and about 10% formal recycling, a different basis; one claims over 90% informal. Sources conflict. — [Factly](https://factly.in/indias-e-waste-generation-doubles-in-8-years-but-processing-remains-skewed); [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/waste/rs-51000-crore-worth-of-materials-in-indias-e-wastemost-slips-through-the-system); [Rajya Sabha PQ](https://rsdebate.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/748973/1/PQ_265_01082024_U1177_p244_p245.pdf)
- Old washing machines fetch about Rs 800-2,500 as scrap from Delhi NCR dealers [ANECDOTAL, blog] — [DigiRake](https://knowledge.digirake.com/uncategorized/sell-old-washing-machine-delhi-2)

### Inferences
- Consumer-side disposal pain (who takes my old fridge, is it certified, do I get fair value) is real but unmeasured; EPR producers need collection and may pay (B2B2C), which is an inference.

### Gaps
- No consumer survey on disposal behaviour in India; no data on EPR take-back usage by households.

## 6. Global comparison

### Takeaway
Cost of repair versus replacement and poor service drive disposal globally; the EU has legislated, the UK publishes brand fault-rate data.

### Cited Findings
- Eurobarometer 2020: 77% of EU consumers would rather repair than buy new but often replace because of cost and poor service — [European Parliament](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/topics/en/article/20220331STO26410/right-to-repair-eu-action-to-make-repairs-more-attractive)
- EU Right to Repair Directive (EU) 2024/1799 in force 30 July 2024; transposition deadline 31 July 2026; extra one-year legal guarantee if repair is chosen; online repair platform; criticised for not addressing repair pricing and for letting sellers replace instead of repair — [European Parliament press](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240419IPR20590/right-to-repair-making-repair-easier-and-more-appealing-to-consumers); [ServiceMag](https://servicemag.org/news/eu-right-to-repair-directive-analysis-2024); [Repair Europe quoted via Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/09/07/how-can-new-eu-rules-save-you-hundreds-on-broken-appliances)
- Euronews 2026 (secondary): only 5% of washing machines and 33% of vacuum cleaners priced so repair makes economic sense; a EUR 280 washer repair can equal a new machine [UNVERIFIED, secondary] — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/09/08/can-the-eu-save-consumers-12bn-on-appliance-repairs)
- Which? UK owner surveys (12,115 members plus 5,430 public, 56,697 products): fridge brand fault rates 3% best vs 19% worst, average 7%; washer-dryer one brand 35% faulty within seven years; full tables are paywalled; data from 2020-21 — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/best-and-worst-large-appliance-brands-new-which-survey-results-revealed-aQufp1S1eeEw); [Which? fridge brands](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/fridges/article/which-fridge-brand/most-reliable-fridge-brands-aAm099G12xlR)

### Inferences
- India lacks an independent brand reliability/service-quality score like Which?; the Repairability Index is the closest policy analogue. A crowd-sourced reliability and service-quality index is a candidate (no revenue obvious; trust asset, possibly lead-gen).

### Gaps
- No US Consumer Reports or academic repair-behaviour study retrieved; Euroconsumers pages found but not opened.
