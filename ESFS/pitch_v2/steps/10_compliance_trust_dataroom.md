# Step 10: Compliance, trust and the data room

**Phase D. Status: FINAL (20 Nov 2026).**

> **Frameworks:** course M3, Pillar 3 (compliance hygiene) and Pillar 4 (data room).
> **Rule (memory):** never claim we are "compliant" with a law before the work is done. Each row below says what is **done [Story]**, **in progress** or **funded by the seed**.

> ### Read this first (flags)
> 1. **The three legal questions an investor will ask first:**
>    - **Is Keepwell Protect insurance?** No. It is a repairs-only service contract that Keepwell fulfils itself. A legal opinion is a seed milestone, and claims money is ring-fenced.
>    - **Are technicians employees?** No. They are per-job partners. We pay the gig-worker levy (modelled at 1.4% of revenue) and carry accident cover.
>    - **Do our installs void the warranty?** No. Installs are done only under brand-authorised service-centre sub-contracts. Unauthorised brands get a referral, not an install.
> 2. **Data:** the appliance record is personal data under the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025. Consent is collected at the shop counter on WhatsApp. Brand data products are aggregated or consented.
> 3. **Safety:** R32 refrigerant is flammable and electrical work carries risk, so there are hard-stop rules in the AI layer, certified technicians only, and job insurance.
> 4. **Data room:** 9 folders, mapped to every pitch claim (§10.3). Items marked [Story] are illustrative for the course.

## 10.4 Weak points

- **The Protect legal opinion is not yet in hand.** It is the first use of seed money, and T&Cs are the fallback. If counsel says "insurance", we sell Protect through a licensed insurer as a corporate agent. Margin then falls to commission, and the stress case's "no Protect" sensitivity applies (Step 8).
- **The gig-worker law is still being notified state by state.** Our 1.4% is an estimate. The levy at 2% costs about 0.6 points of margin.
- **DPDP Rules timelines** (consent managers, data-protection officer) apply as the volume of records grows. Budgeted from Round 2.
