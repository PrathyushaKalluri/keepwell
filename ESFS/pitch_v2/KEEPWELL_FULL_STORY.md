# Keepwell: the full story

> **Illustrative case** for the IIIT Hyderabad ESFS course pitch. Founder histories, the company timeline, the pilot results and the financials are [Story]: assumptions kept consistent with each other and generated from the same model as the plan (`keepwell_model.xlsx`). ## 1. Ravi's Thursday

Ravi runs a multi-brand appliance shop in Guntur. On Monday he sells a ₹38,000 AC to a family he has known for ten years. The brand's installer says two to five days, so the family calls Ravi, and calls again. On Thursday, in 40-degree heat, they are standing at his counter, not the brand's.

Two years later the AC fails. It is out of warranty, like 80–91% of the appliances in Indian homes. A local mechanic guesses at the fault, charges what he likes and keeps no record; 44% of AC service goes this way. Seven of the twelve households we surveyed keep no service record at all. The ones that do keep it on paper: an agency's sticker on the purifier with the next date in marker, a handwritten repair diary, a refill date written on the gas cylinder.

Ravi nets 2–3% on the sale and earns nothing afterwards. Yet consumer commissions have held sellers jointly liable with brands. **The person the family trusts has no way to keep the promise he made at the counter.**

## 2. The insight

In small-town India, **the shop owns the trust**, but its role ends at the counter. The people who can fix things, local technicians and brand service centres, are never connected to it, and no one keeps the appliance's record. Connect them under the shop's name and three things follow:
- the family stays with the shop
- technicians get steady work
- every appliance gets a life record

## 4. Keepwell

**Keepwell is the after-sale service arm of every small-town appliance shop. The shop sells the promise; Keepwell keeps it.**

- **Authorised installation within 24 hours**, done under brand-authorised sub-contracts so the warranty stays safe.
- **Guaranteed repair and AMC** at a fixed rate card, for the AC, refrigerator, washing machine and water purifier.
- **Keepwell Protect:** a 2-year repair plan the shop sells on its own bill (about ₹2,000). It is a repairs-only service contract that Keepwell fulfils itself, not insurance, and expected claims are ring-fenced.
- **The appliance record:** every visit, part and plan, consented on WhatsApp.

**How a job runs.** It all happens on WhatsApp and is run by Keepwell's AI desk.
1. The shop bills the install and Protect.
2. The record and plan certificate are created from photos.
3. An authorised technician installs within a day.
4. Months later, a Telugu voice note triages the fault and the part to carry, and checks the plan cover.
5. The technician confirms the fault, closes with a photo and an OTP, and the record updates.

**What the AI may and may not do.** It reads and drafts. Rules and people decide prices, safety stops, claim rejections and refunds.

## 6. The economics (Step 7b model)

| | Revenue | Contribution / EBITDA | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One mature shop** | ₹3.5 L service + ₹0.9 L Protect | ₹1.16 L contribution | The shop itself earns about ₹3,170 a month from us, up from ₹1.7k in the pilot, because we pay it for the sale it controls |
| **One mature 60-shop cluster** | ₹2.72 Cr | ₹44 L EBITDA (16%) on service | Breaks even at 22 shops |
| **The company** | ₹130.5 Cr in year 7 | EBITDA-positive in year 5; ₹8.6 Cr (6.6%) in year 7 | While we open 15+ clusters a year, data (verified installs and claims, leads) is what tips the company into profit |

**At the mature run-rate,** the network earns 13.4%, of which 9.1% comes from service alone. We plan on the **stress case**: 5% Protect attach, 45% claims and 50 shops per cluster.

**From year 3, existing service centres run our new clusters as partners.**
- **What they get:** 10% of the cluster's core service revenue, plus their technicians' per-job pay. That fills their idle off-season capacity.
- **What we keep:** the shops, the customer, the payment, Protect and the record.
- **What we save:** we spend ₹1 L a month per partner cluster instead of funding a ₹23 L-a-year cluster team.

**Nobody owns the independent shop's after-sale relationship.** Our moat is exclusive shop agreements, density, authorisation and the record.

**Keepwell keeps the promise the shop made when it sold the appliance.**
