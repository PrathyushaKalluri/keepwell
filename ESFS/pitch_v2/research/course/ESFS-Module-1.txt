Build a Product / Service
     Why do startups get launched?
           Solve unsolved problems
           Disrupt existing solutions
     Building the right offerings (product/service) is key to startup existence.
     Investments are required in prototyping, development, features, etc.
     For technology products, UI/UX, security, and quality also become paramount.


          Typically, 40% of funding needs is towards building the product.


IIIT-H | ESFS Course | 2026                                                         14
Build a Team
     A strong team is critical for the success of a Startup.
     To build a disruptive business, one needs the best talent.
     A combination of cash and ESOPs necessary to attract a strong team.
     The ideal option is for founders to get people known to them.
           Personal credibility over company brand necessary in early stages


          Typically, 25% of early-stage Startup funding goes toward team building.


IIIT-H | ESFS Course | 2026                                                          15
Discover Product-Market Fit
     The most important discovery in a startup journey is product-market fit.
           Who will pay for the product or service?
           Why will they pay for such a product or service?
           How much will they pay and when?
           Will my product or service actually solve the problem?
     Finding this is typically an iterative process and requires investment.


          Product-Market Fit investments are generally invisible, but are critical to success.


IIIT-H | ESFS Course | 2026                                                                      16
Reach the Market
     Businesses spend ~20% of revenue on marketing and distribution.
           This can be higher for B2C businesses and lower for B2B businesses.
           Expenditures tend to be higher initially on lower base, optimised over time with scale.
     Taking the product/service to a wider audience is key to startup success.
     Key expenditures in this category include
           Branding, Advertisements, Lead Generation, Team, Advisors, etc.
     The idea that "I have a great product, people will automatically buy it" is a myth


          Companies spend 5-40% of revenues on marketing and distribution.

Investors
    Provide the capital runway to build, test, and scale your startup
    Multiple category of Investors are in the market, Angel, VC, PE etc.
         Each category focuses on different stage of the Company Stage
         Eg. Angels (Indian Angel Network), Family Offices (Premji Invest), VCs (Sequoia Surge), Corporate VCs (Reliance Ventures,
         Nexus), PE (True North), Debt (InCred Ventures, Banks, NSDC)
    Most offer value beyond money: mentorship, governance, strategic introductions


IIIT-H | ESFS Course | 2026                                                                                                      27
Government
    Shapes policy, reduces risk, and provides non-dilutive funding
    Central schemes: Startup India Seed Fund, Fund of Funds (SIDBI), CGSS loan guarantee
    State programs: Telangana T-Hub, WE Hub grants, APEDA export incentives
    Supports across stages: grants for idea/MVP, pilot schemes for PMF, guarantees for scaling
    Also offers tax breaks, procurement channels (GeM), and regulatory easing
