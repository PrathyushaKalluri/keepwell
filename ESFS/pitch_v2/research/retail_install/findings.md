## I2. Who pays for an AC installation, how much, and is it "free"?
The customer pays the brand's authorised technician directly. A split-AC installation costs about ₹1,200–1,500 plus GST, with materials extra. "Free installation" appears only as a promotion. Confidence: High.

**Brand rate cards:**
- **LG:** ₹1,199 for a split AC, ₹500 for a window AC. Copper with insulation ₹1,050 a metre, bracket ₹750, drain pipe ₹90 a metre, site inspection ₹400 ([LG price charter, Feb 2026](https://lg.com/in/support/rac-installation-price-charter)).
- **Lloyd:** ₹1,199 plus 18% GST as "Maximum Applicable Rates". Copper ₹950 a metre, bracket ₹750 ([Havells](https://havells.com/service-charge)).
- **Daikin:** ₹1,500 (₹1,770 with GST). Copper ₹1,100 a metre ([Daikin](https://www.daikinindia.com/node/595)).
- **Panasonic:** ₹1,200; copper ₹850 a metre ([leaflet, Mar 2025](https://store.in.panasonic.com/media/wysiwyg/pdf-guide/NON-STANDARD-AC-LEAFLET.pdf)). Its free installation applies only through a Panasonic authorised service centre.
- **Voltas:** ₹1,199 split, ₹499 window. Low confidence.
- **Samsung:** "discounted installation" on selected models.
- **Other categories:** a Havells geyser costs ₹250. Aquaguard and Kent water purifiers are installed free inside the plan. Washing machine and TV installation: UNKNOWN.

**Market range:**
- Flipkart: "₹1,500 to ₹3,500" plus materials, with hidden costs at the door ([Mar 2026](https://stories.flipkart.com/announcement/flipkart-expands-smart-cooling-portfolio-with-motorola-air-conditioners-with-transparent-installation-model)).
- Urban Company: from ₹799 on its base page; ₹1,499–1,899 on city pages.

**Share of AC sales with free installation:** UNKNOWN.

**Booking:**
- **Customer books:** LG, Panasonic, Samsung, Kent.
- **Dealer books:** Carrier needs a dealer ticket; Daikin and IFB need a dealer stamp.
- **E-commerce books:** Flipkart's seller arranges it, through the brand partner or Jeeves.

**Delays:**
- LG: 24–72 hours. Flipkart: 2–5 days.
- Panasonic: a 2-day no-show ([Smartprix, Apr 2026](https://www.smartprix.com/bytes/panasonic-india-customer-spends-two-days-waiting-for-ac-installation-that-never-showed-up/)).
- Tier 2/3 delays: UNKNOWN.

**What the retailer earns from installation:** UNKNOWN. There are hints of a margin on materials.

## I3. Does third-party installation void the warranty? Yes, for ACs (High)
- **Samsung:** void if installed by anyone not authorised ([PDF](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/assets/in/tnc/ac_warranty_terms_and_conditions.pdf)).
- **Daikin:** void unless installed by Daikin's authorised dealer or representative ([Daikin](https://daikinindia.com/node/34556)).
- **Carrier Midea:** the warranty is "automatically terminated" if a third person installs.
- **IFB:** void if installed by unauthorised personnel.
- **Panasonic:** the compressor warranty is void if anyone unauthorised attends to the unit.
- **Godrej:** must be installed by a Godrej-authorised provider.
- **LG:** "may void".
- **Voltas, Blue Star, Lloyd, Whirlpool:** UNKNOWN.

**Related evidence:**
- Flipkart (Oct 2026) and Reliance Digital accept returns only when the brand's authorised installer did the job.
- Forums: the warranty is activated by the authorised installer registering the unit.

**Implication:** an independent provider has no paid wedge into in-warranty ACs unless it is brand-authorised, or works as the authorised dealer's workforce.

## I4 / S7. How many independent retailers are there?
**Brand reach:**
- LG: 35,640 touchpoints, including 30,726 distributors and sub-dealers and 1,134 multi-brand outlets (RHP).
- Voltas: 30,000+. Blue Star: 10,000+ outlets in 900 towns.
- Havells (260k) and V-Guard (100k) are mostly electrical shops.

**General trade's share of sales:** about 40% for appliances (Redseer, Jul 2025). Blue Star room ACs about 46% (derived). Voltas about 65% (derived).

**Derived counts (Low confidence):**
- Unique independent large-appliance dealers: 30k / 45k / 70k (LOW / BASE / HIGH). The pitch's 78k has no source.
- Dealers selling more than 60 major units a month: **5k / 9k / 18k**.

**Method:**
1. About 14M ACs × 46–65% general trade ÷ 45k dealers gives 12–17 ACs per dealer per month.
2. ACs are about 25–35% of a dealer's major-appliance units, so the average is about 35–65 major units a month.
3. With a skewed size distribution, 15–30% of dealers sell more than 60.

**Result:** the pitch's 23,400 is above even the HIGH case. At BASE, the TAM is about ₹1,550 Cr, not ₹4,000 Cr.

## I1 / S2 / F13 / S8. Retailer economics and partnering
**Gross margins:**
- Aditya Vision 15.6%. EMIL 14.3%, rising to 17.2%. Sathya 16.84%, with net profit of 1.32%.
- Independent dealers: about 10–15% (Low confidence).
- Net margin about 2–3%. A 60-unit shop nets about ₹36–54k a month (DERIVED), so a ₹3,500 commission does matter to it.

**Extended-warranty commission in India:** UNKNOWN.
- At EMIL, about a third of extended-warranty revenue becomes profit before tax.
- IRDAI's 2026 proposals may cap commission on extended warranties that are sold as insurance.
- **The pitch's 5% and 8% commissions are low against these benchmarks.**

## I12. Why now
- **AC penetration:** about 8–12% of households. ICAP projects about 40% in 20 years.
- **Voltas:** 85% of buyers are first-time buyers.
- **Blue Star:** 65% of sales from Tier III–V towns, which are "outpacing Tier 1 and 2".
- **AC market size:** ₹53,600 Cr (CY25) growing to ₹93,610 Cr (CY29).
- **GST on ACs:** cut from 28% to 18% in Sep 2025.
- **Headwinds:** demand depends on the weather (−25% in Apr–Jun 2025), and Flipkart and the brands are standardising installation themselves.

## What it means for the business model
1. **Installation is not a paid wedge on in-warranty ACs** unless we are brand-authorised, or work under the dealer's authorised status.
2. If we are authorised, installation economics come from the brand's payout plus margin on materials (payout UNKNOWN).
3. Installs outside ACs are free or small.
4. **There are probably 5k–18k serviceable retailers**, so the TAM roughly halves.
5. Retailers compare everything to extended-warranty economics. Relief from liability and complaints is the stronger pitch to them.
6. Out-of-warranty repair and AMC stay open.
7. Trade-ins compete with Digi2L, Attero, Cashify and Flipkart.

## Killer facts
- **K1.** The warranty-void clauses (Samsung, Daikin, Carrier, IFB, Godrej, Panasonic).

- **K5.** Flipkart bundles installation into the price; LG promises 24–72 hours.
