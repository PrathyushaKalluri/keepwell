## 1. Regional trade-in network
**Who runs exchanges today:**
- LG's exchange is run by Attero (Selsmart). Lloyd's any-brand Eco Exchange launched with Selsmart in Jan 2026. Bosch has used Digi2L since 2023 (FACT).
- **Who funds the exchange bonus: UNKNOWN.** Croma offers up to ₹8,500 on ACs under brand terms, which points to brands funding it (PROXY).

**LG/Attero price card:**

| Unit | Working | Dead |
|---|---|---|
| Split AC | ₹5,000 | ₹4,500 |
| Window AC | ₹4,000 | ₹3,500 |
| Fridge, double door 250–350 L | ₹2,000 | ₹1,100 |
| Washing machine, top-load | ₹1,200 | ₹900 |

**Key insight (DERIVED).** A dead AC fetches ₹4,500 as an exchange but ₹3,500 as scrap. The ₹1,000 gap is about one EPR certificate (≈40 kg × ₹22/kg), which recycler-owned operators can collect. So Keepwell should be the **field arm of Selsmart, Recykal or Digi2L**, not set its own prices.

**Players:**
- **Selsmart:** ₹100 Cr run-rate on 30k+ orders a month; target ₹500 Cr by Mar 2026, outcome UNKNOWN (FACT).
- **Digi2L:** small, backed by Onsitego.
- **Cashify:** FY25 revenue ₹1,096 Cr with a ₹10.5 Cr loss; an IPO is planned for early 2027 (FACT).

**Exchange share of sales: UNKNOWN.** Flipkart's 2021 forecast was 20%.

**Logistics:**
- A one-off pickup in a Tier 2 town costs about ₹500 (Porter base ₹200–230 + ₹14–18/km + helper).
- If the old unit is collected during the new install and runs are batched, it costs about ₹60–100 a unit plus about ₹100 of technician time (DERIVED).

**Unit economics, asset-light (per unit):**

| | LOW | BASE | HIGH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fee or spread (ASSUMPTION) | ₹250 | ₹400 | ₹600 |
| Cost (DERIVED) | ₹250 | ₹170 | ₹130 |
| Contribution | ₹0 | ₹230 | ₹470 |

**Capital:** a payout float of ₹3–8 L per cluster.

**Market (DERIVED):**
- About 35 mn large units sold a year × 10–20% exchanged = 3.5–7 mn exchanges.
- Value of the devices: ₹875–1,750 Cr. Fee pool: ₹105–210 Cr.

**First ₹1 Cr:** year 1–2 on gross revenue; year 3–4 on net, at about 12 clusters.

## 2. Spare parts: the strongest fit
**Market (DERIVED):** home-appliance repair is about ₹8,140 Cr (BlueWeave 2023). Parts make up 30–60% of a bill, so the out-of-warranty parts pool is **₹2,400–4,900 Cr**.

**Customer prices (Urban Company):**
- capacitor ₹599–749
- fan motor ₹2,299
- PCB repair ₹1,500–4,500
- 1.5T compressor ₹8,500

**Margins:** distributor margin is UNKNOWN. The proxy is a 15–40% dealer margin.

**Market structure:**
- Brands may restrict genuine parts to their authorised service centres, as Samsung does.
- Right to Repair is still voluntary in India.

**Per cluster (DERIVED):**
- 12,480 jobs × 40% need parts × ₹1,500 average = **₹75 L parts revenue a year**.
- Gross margin 15–25% = ₹11–19 L. Contribution after holding, obsolescence and returns: 8–12%.

**Working capital:** about ₹10 L of fast-moving stock per cluster. One regional stock room covers 3–5 clusters.

**Comparables:**
- India has no appliance-parts pure-play. The auto-parts analogue Koovers had FY25 revenue of ₹198 Cr and a ₹36 Cr loss.
- Global: Marcone (Genstar buyout, 2021) and Reliable Parts (bolt-on acquisitions in 2025). These are private-equity roll-ups.

**First ₹1 Cr:** year 1–2 for revenue; year 3 for gross profit.

**Network advantage:** the appliance record predicts the part before the visit. Buying is pooled across clusters, and failure-by-model data becomes a product.

## 3. Refurbished marketplace
**Resale prices (PROXY):**
- refurbished split AC from ₹17,999 including installation
- used split AC ₹10–16k
- refurbished fridge about ₹5.5k
- washing machine: UNKNOWN

**Unit economics (DERIVED):**
- **AC:** cost ₹8.75–10.45k, sold at ₹12–18k, for a gross margin of 13–51% (base about 25%).
- **Fridge:** cost about ₹3.5–4k, sold at ₹5.5k, for a gross margin of 28–37%.
- **Marketplace model:** Back Market takes about 12.6–14% of GMV.

**Capital:** ₹7–9 L of stock per cluster plus a ₹3–5 L workshop. AC buying and selling both peak in Mar–Jun.

**Market:** ₹600–2,600 Cr (DERIVED).

**Comparables:**
- **India:** Quikr bought Zefo for about ₹200 Cr (2019); it is now dormant.
- **Back Market:** GMV $3.5 bn in 2025 and EBITDA-positive; $5.7 bn valuation in 2022.
- **Refurbed:** revenue €93M in 2025.
- **Brand programmes:** Beko and Bosch run refurbishment schemes.

**Risks:**
- warranty comebacks
- old R-22 units (HCFC servicing ends 2030)
- small-town buyers prefer buying from each other
- GST margin scheme
- stock tied up in the off-season

**Timing:** year 3+, once trade-ins supply the units.

## 4. E-waste collection
**Who earns:** EPR certificates go to registered recyclers; collectors get none.

**Certificate prices:**
- The price band is ₹22–74/kg (FACT), against about ₹7/kg before the floor price.
- The floor is being challenged in the Delhi High Court by Havells, Daikin, Voltas, Blue Star, LG and Samsung. There was an interim stay in Dec 2025. Status in Nov 2026: UNKNOWN.

**Keepwell's share:** a handling fee of about ₹2–6/kg, so ₹80–240 a unit (ASSUMPTION).

**Players:**
- Attero: FY25 revenue ₹961 Cr, EBITDA 3.2%.
- Recykal: FY26 revenue ₹1,498 Cr, valued at about $280M.
- Karo Sambhav: about ₹150 Cr.
- Namo eWaste: SME IPO.
- Ecoreco: about 18–19× revenue.

**Refrigerant credits:** none can be banked today.

**Verdict:** e-waste is a feeder and goodwill for brands, **not a profit layer.**

## 5. AI manufacturing inspection: avoid
**Who would buy:**
- Amber (FY26 revenue ₹12,186 Cr), EPACK, PG Electroplast.
- PLI for white goods: 85 firms.

**Adoption:** use of AI vision in Indian appliance plants is UNKNOWN.

**Comparables:**
- **SwitchOn:** $13M raised, about $2M revenue (LOW).
- Others: Frinks, Instrumental, Elementary, Landing AI.

**Why avoid it:** a different buyer, different skills, long sales cycles and funded specialists already in place.

**Field-failure data is only a partial advantage:**
- Brands already hold their warranty-claim data.
- Our data is small-town and out-of-warranty.
- Linking it to factory serial and BOM records is controlled by the brand.

**What to do instead:** sell **reliability analytics to brands**. Don't build cameras.
