# Keepwell — Investor Q&A bank
> **Illustrative case.** Founder histories, the company timeline, the pilot results and the financials are assumptions made for the IIIT Hyderabad ESFS course pitch. They are kept consistent with the team repo and with each other.

Each answer is at most two sentences. Say it, then point to the appendix slide. Source: `../steps/12_storyboard_redteam.md` §12.4.

| # | Question | Answer | Slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why would an agency pay to make fewer trips if a second visit earns a second fee? | 13 of 18 owners say repeat visits are mostly unpaid, so they're a cost. Recovered jobs alone (₹2,400 a month, measured) cover about two-thirds of our fee. | A3, A5 |
| 2 | 14 agencies after 9 months is slow. | Deliberate: we proved price, accuracy and retention before paying for a field team, and grew ~19% a month with no salespeople. The seed buys the sales team. | A4 |
| 3 | Founder-led sales won't scale. | Our COO closed 8 agencies at ~25% of his time, which is a full-time rep's ~6–7 a month. E5 measures it in the first two quarters. | A6 |
| 4 | What stops Urban Company or Servify copying you? | They can copy features, not 8,200 verified repair outcomes or a record that follows the machine. Their models take the customer or serve the brand, the opposite of arming the agency. | 8, A13 |
| 5 | Your agency count isn't official. | No one has one. Ours is triangulated from the government's technician estimate, the repair-jobs count and the ₹ market, and all three agree. | A1 |
| 6 | Which line takes you past ₹100 Cr? | Tier 1 plus plans and claims at record scale reach ~₹66 Cr by year 8. ₹100 Cr+ needs price reviews and the next categories, and we say so. | A10 |
| 7 | ₹23.75 Cr post is ~34x your revenue. | Seed rounds are priced on comparable rounds and on your required return. At ₹19 Cr pre, your own VC maths returns 35%. | A10 |
| 8 | Telugu speech recognition is bad on phone lines. | 21% raw word error rate, 14% with our lexicon after 9 months. We read back every note and abstain and ask 16% of the time. | A4 |
| 9 | What if the AI misses a gas leak? | It can't release a hazardous job: the hard-stop is a rule layer outside the model, and safety calls are human-only. 0 safety incidents in 9 months. | 15, A14 |
| 10 | Gemini prices double on 1 Jan 2027. | We budget at 2027 prices (₹4.5 a job against ₹3.0 measured today). AI cost moves LTV:CAC by only ±0.4x. | A7 |
| 11 | Can you legally sell warranty plans? | We don't underwrite. We distribute insurer-filed plans through an IRDAI-registered route, on commission. | A14 |
| 12 | Why ₹4.75 Cr and 24 months? | The gates land around months 13–15, and a Series A takes 4–6 months. ₹4.0 Cr (21 months) is our minimum close. | A9 |
| 13 | Are the founders committed? | Full-time since May 2025, ₹15L of our own money in, ₹50k stipends for 13 months, vesting with a cliff, IP assigned. The CEO's FixNear buyer isn't a competitor. | A11 |
| 14 | Why only one investor? | A lead wants clean 15–20% ownership, and fewer holders keep later rounds fast. Strategic people join as ESOP advisors; we ask the lead to reserve for the Series A. | A11 |
| 15 | If an agency churns, what happens to its data? | De-identified outcomes stay in Mistri Gyan under the signed agency licence; personal data goes back to the agency; households keep their own record. | A14 |
| 16 | Payback of 6.3 months misses your own 6-month gate. | We re-based it openly once we modelled observed volumes (125 jobs). 6.3 months is well inside the ≤ 12-month SMB SaaS norm. | A7 |
