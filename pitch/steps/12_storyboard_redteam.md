## 12.1 How this step is built
**Course frame (M3, Pillar 1).**
- **Deck structure:** why this startup · market pain, solution, opportunity · team, traction, X-factor · the ask (amount, milestones, rationale) · consistent evidence links.
- **Audience:** VCs want "scale, traction, team".
- **Avoid:** hiding weak metrics, jargon, claims without proof, an unclear ask, an incoherent story.

**Our choices:**
- One idea runs through the deck: ***every repair starts from zero, and Keepwell remembers.***
- Each number appears once, where it works hardest.
- Weak points are shown, not hidden: the ₹100 arm, only 14 agencies, self-reported owner time, payback re-based to 7 months.
- **16 main slides, ~11 minutes**, plus an appendix for Q&A. The detailed slide-by-slide content is refined with the team before presenting (team note).

## 12.7 Findings and fixes
**Pass 1 (independent reviewer)**

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The base-case IRR was below the 35% hurdle; the venture scale was unclear | The VC method uses the funded plan; **the price is never above the VC-method price**; the path to ₹100 Cr was added |
| 2 | The moat proof came from a feature described as "not live" | A5 is live in production (Mar 2026); the gate was passed in Aug 2026; the gain is attributed by source |
| 3 | The timeline compressed angel, pilot and seed into weeks | **The company was backdated** (incorporated May 2025, angels Oct 2025, paid pilot Feb 2026, seed now) |
| 4 | Numbers disagreed between documents | Full regeneration from the model |
| 5 | The value ≥ 2x gate failed at modelled ARPA | Value split into measured vs self-reported; gate ≥ 1.5x and net ≥ ₹3,000 |
| 6 | Weak metrics were softened | Revenue and credits shown as real figures; retention gate ≥ 97% |
| 7 | The plans pool was inflated | Appliances only; IRDAI route named |
| 8 | Data rights | Step 10.2E; licence signed by all 14 agencies |
| 9 | Founder commitment | Full-time since May 2025; FixNear buyer not a competitor |
| 10 | The slide 2 villain was our customer | Ramesh's beat: "nobody is the villain" |

**Pass 2 (full pitch)**

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Model used 145 jobs/agency while the pilot showed 125 | **Observed 125 used everywhere.** Revenue −14%; price ₹19 Cr |
| 2 | Value claims mixed measured and self-reported | Split everywhere; measured value alone is 1.23x the fee |
| 3 | Rep productivity (80 a year) had no evidence | Backed by the COO's part-time pace; E5 |
| 4 | Venture scale (low ARPA) | "The record is the business": revenue per agency +33% by Y5; price headroom (value ~1.8x what agencies pay) |
| 5 | ₹30L over 18 months was unexplained | Itemised (₹34L, Step 2.4); key-person risk answered |
| 6 | Desk Pro 25% had no evidence | Observed 4 of 14 (29%) |
| 7 | "The lean plan breaks even without a Series A" was false | Corrected: **both plans need a Series A; growth needs less outside cash** |
| 8 | Rounding pushed the price above the hurdle | Rule: never above the VC-method price |
| 9 | Payback 6.3 months failed the 6-month gate | Re-based to ≤ 7 months openly (SMB norm ≤ 12) |
| 10 | Syndicate complexity | **One lead VC**; strategic people as ESOP advisors |
| 11 | Public repo with real names | **Disclosure line at the top of every document** (team may also make the repo private) |
