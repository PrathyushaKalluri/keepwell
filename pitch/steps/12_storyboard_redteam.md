## 12.1 How this step is built
**Course frame (M3, Pillar 1).**
- **The deck structure:** *Why this startup (impact) · market pain, solution, opportunity · team, traction, X-factor · the ask (amount, milestones, rationale) · consistent evidence links.*
- **Know the audience:** VCs want "scale, traction, team".
- **Bring data into the narrative:** TAM/SAM/SOM with figures, testimonials and pipeline, NPS and repeat use.
- **Communicating the edge:** the Big Why · who's in the cockpit · the opportunity · what's unique · how we'll achieve it · why we'll succeed.
- **Avoid:** hiding weak metrics, jargon, claim without proof, an unclear ask, an incoherent story.

**Our choices** (decided here, as Steps 5–9 deferred them):
- One idea runs through the deck: ***every repair starts from zero — Keepwell remembers.***
- Every number appears **once**, on the slide where it works hardest.
- Weak points are *shown*: the ₹100 arm, the 74% vs 75% gate, break-even in Y5. The course flags hiding weak metrics as a mistake.
- **16 main slides, ~11 minutes**, plus an appendix for Q&A.

**Why this order:**
- **Slides 2–4 are the "Big Why"** (a human story, then scale, then insight).
- **Slides 5–6 are "What is the opportunity / what we do".**
- **7–8 are "Why we will succeed"** (traction before claims, so the moat lands as proven, not promised).
- **9–11 are "Why now and how big"** (the market comes after traction, so it reads as earned).
- **12–13 are the engine.**
- **14–15 are "Who is in the cockpit" and hygiene.**
- **16 is the ask**, closing on the vision line from Step 1.
