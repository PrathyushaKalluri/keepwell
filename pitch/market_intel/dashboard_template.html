Assumptions behind the estimate
Class
Classification rules
  • INSUFFICIENT DATA: LOW confidence and no verified chain outlets. Missing listings are never read as a gap.
  • COMPETITIVE / SATURATED: ≥3 competitors verified active, or ≥8 verified+claimed (only 30 cities checked).
  • HIGH OPPORTUNITY: top 15% entry score, confidence not LOW, ≥2 chain outlets and ≥3 listed repair points.
  • PROMISING BUT UNVERIFIED: top 40% entry score otherwise.
  • LOW COMMERCIAL ATTRACTIVENESS: the rest.

Recommended model per city is the same everywhere for now: retailer-referred post-warranty jobs fulfilled by local agencies, tested in a 90-day pilot. Rank columns under alternative weights show stability.

Preset
GTV (what customers pay) is not revenue. Revenue = labour margin kept after paying the agency + parts margin + booking fee. Contribution = revenue − retailer commission − payment fees − refunds/rework − support − coordination. Defaults: average bill from the demand model; all other inputs are ASSUMPTIONS except support cost (Urban Company disclosure proxy).
