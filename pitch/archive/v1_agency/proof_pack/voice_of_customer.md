> **Illustrative case.** Founder histories, the company timeline, the pilot results and the financials are assumptions made for the IIIT Hyderabad ESFS course pitch. They are kept consistent with the team repo and with each other.

## Households: "nothing in a repair can be checked" [Real]
> "They are never going to say the price, they're going to just give you an estimation… three thousand to five thousand." — **Sai Kakki**, young professional, Hyderabad

> "Even a duplicate part will have 3 months of warranty and will work for 3 months at least. We won't know if the part is duplicate or original." — **Amma**, homemaker, Andhra Pradesh

> "If we visit a service station run by the company… whatever they say I will listen. If we visit a local man definitely doubts will arise." — **Rk Sir**, retired professional

> "I had a small door repair. When I asked him to come, he came and did it immediately. He took the amount genuinely. Since then, I have been able to trust him and approve his small works." — **Sarala Aunty**, homeowner who manages two properties in other cities through one tradesman

> "Back to back we have to call them… whether they are coming or not." — household interviewee

**Survey (19 responses, 12 qualified) [Real]:**
- In **7 of 12** repairs, the person deciding, approving, paying and opening the door were not the same.
- **7 of 12** keep **no service record**.
- **47%** of 110 coded public complaints are about no-shows and chasing.

## Agency owners: 18 interviews, Jan–Apr 2025 [Story, Step 6]
- **0 of 18** use any field-service software; 16 of 18 run on phone and WhatsApp only.
- About **1 in 8 requests is lost** (calls missed on a job, voice notes never followed up).
- **13 of 18** say a repeat visit for the same complaint is mostly unpaid.
- **14 of 18** prefer paying per completed job; median acceptable ₹30–50.

## Our paying agencies, Nov 2026 [Story, Step 6]
- **11 of 14** owners would be "very disappointed" if Circuit went away.
- Price test, the ₹100-arm owner who halved usage: *"It takes too much of a small job."* This is why we price at ₹40.
- 2 of the last 10 agencies joined because another owner referred them.
