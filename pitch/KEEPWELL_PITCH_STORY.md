# Keepwell — How the Story Is Told
> **Illustrative case.** Founder histories, the company timeline, the pilot results and the financials are assumptions made for the IIIT Hyderabad ESFS course pitch. They are kept consistent with the team repo and with each other.

*The whole seed pitch in one document: what we say, in what order, with which numbers, and why. Built from the step documents in [`steps/`](steps/), the model [`keepwell_model.xlsx`](keepwell_model.xlsx) and the deck in [`deck/`](deck/).*

## 1. The pitch in 60 seconds
> Every year India does **15 crore appliance repairs**. Most are done by **37,500 small agencies** that run on a phone and a notebook. Calls get missed, technicians go in blind, and nothing about the machine is written down: **₹550 Cr of wasted trips** a year, and **27 crore machines with no memory**.
>
> **Keepwell** is the care layer for everything a home owns: one living record per machine, with a product at every stage of its life. We start with **Circuit**, an AI desk inside the agency's own WhatsApp. It understands a Telugu voice note, ranks the likely root cause and the part to carry, quotes from the agency's rate card, and learns every time a technician verifies the fix.
>
> Since February 2026, **14 agencies pay us** (only one has left):
> - one-visit closure up from **71% to 83%**, held for six months
> - **96%** of requests now become jobs
> - our root-cause accuracy has climbed from **62% to 78%** as verified repairs teach it
>
> We charge **₹40, only when the fix works**: about 7% of the job, against the 28% a platform keeps. We are raising **₹4.75 Cr** to take Circuit from 14 agencies to 260 and reach Series A from strength. Over time, Keepwell becomes **the record everyone checks before touching a machine.**

## 2. The one idea that runs through everything
***Every repair starts from zero. Keepwell remembers.***

Every slide serves this idea:
- **The problem:** no memory.
- **The insight:** trust is in people because nothing can be checked.
- **The product:** a prepared visit plus a record.
- **The moat:** verified outcomes compound.
- **The business:** the record unlocks plans, claims and parts.

## 5. The story, slide by slide
**Timing:** ~11 minutes, 16 slides.
**Who speaks:**
- **CEO (Prathyusha):** opens and closes
- **CTO (Sri Charan):** product and trust
- **COO (Kavyanth):** market, model and team

*The exact slide content is refined with the team before presenting.*

### Act 1: Why this matters (slides 1–4, ~2:45)
**Slide 1 · Title (CEO, 0:15)**
- **Say:** "We're Keepwell. We're raising ₹4.75 crore to give every machine in an Indian home a memory, starting with the agencies who fix them."

**Slide 2 · The ₹350 part (CEO, 1:00)**
- **On screen:** *"A ₹350 part. A ₹38,000 new AC. Between them sits a repair system with no memory."*
- **Say:**
  1. Ramagundam, a 46 °C afternoon. A retired miner's AC stops cooling.
  2. Ramesh, the agency owner, is up a ladder on another job. His phone rings 14 times, and the voice note is never written down.
  3. The son in Bengaluru opens a shopping app: ₹38,000, three days.
  4. The fault was a ₹350 capacitor.
- **Land:** *"Nobody here is the villain. The system just has no memory."* Then Sri Charan's line: *"Our machines cost my family ₹10,000 a month, never evenly."*

**Slide 3 · The problem (CEO, 0:50)**
- **On screen:** *"15 crore repairs a year. ₹550 Cr of wasted trips. 27 crore machines with no memory. And the 37,500 agencies that do the work run on a phone and a notebook."*
- **Say** the two sides: the agency loses about 1 in 8 requests and goes in blind; the household can't verify anything (55% got a defective machine; only 21% were made whole).
- **Caveat, once:** ₹550 Cr assumes the global 77% first-visit fix rate.

**Slide 4 · The insight (CEO, 0:40)**
- **On screen:** *"Nothing in a repair can be checked, so people trust the person."*
- **Quote:** Sarala Aunty: *"He came and did it immediately… since then I have been able to trust him and approve his small works."*
- **Say:** "That's why 44% go local, up from 38%, and 76% avoid brand service."
- **Land:** *"We don't replace the trusted agency. We make it the smartest one in town."*

### Act 2: What we're building, and the proof (slides 5–8, ~3:30)
**Slide 5 · The vision (CEO, 0:45)**
- **On screen:** *"The CIBIL of household machines."*
- **Say:** "Every stage of a machine's life starts from zero. Keepwell holds one record per machine and puts a product at each stage. We start where the pain, the payer and the truth meet: the repair."

**Slide 6 · Circuit (CTO, 1:00)**
- **On screen:** the 7-step flow and the 20-second clip.
- **Land:** *"We only earn when the technician verifies the fix, and that same moment teaches the system."*

**Slide 7 · Traction (CTO, 1:00)**
- **On screen:** *"Nine months of paid use. 14 agencies. One churned."*
- **Say:** "14 paying agencies, about 8,200 jobs, one-visit closure from 71% to 83% for six months, and 96% of requests captured. Root-cause accuracy went from 62% to 78% and passed our 75% gate in August. Since June the gains come mostly from verified repairs. We tested price too: at ₹100 one agency halved its usage, so we price at ₹40."
- **Pre-empt:** "Why only 14? Because we proved price, accuracy and retention before paying for a sales team. The seed buys that team."

**Slide 8 · Why it compounds (CTO, 0:45)**
- **On screen:** *"Anyone can copy the AI. No one can copy verified repairs."*
- **Say:** "Others can copy features in a quarter. They can't copy verified outcomes, a record that follows the machine, technician-confirmed parts data, or our Telugu corrections. All 14 agencies have signed the licence that keeps de-identified outcomes with us."

### Act 3: Why now, how big, who else (slides 9–11, ~1:55)
**Slide 9 · Why now (COO, 0:30)**
1. AI at ₹4.5 a job against a ₹40 fee.
2. Only 1 in 10 homes has an AC, and India needs ~5x more AC technicians by 2037.
3. Repair is opening up.
4. DPDP 2027 rewards consent-first records.

**Slide 10 · Market (COO, 0:45)**
- **On screen:** *"Circuit is the wedge. The record is the business."* The funnel: 37,500 → 22,500 → 1,400 → 120. The pools: ₹158–396 Cr (Circuit) and ₹1,005–2,370 Cr (Keepwell).
- **Say:** "No official agency count exists. Ours comes from the government's technician estimate, agrees with 15 crore jobs and the ₹9,000 crore market, and lands at the 125 jobs a month our own agencies show."

**Slide 11 · Competition (COO, 0:40)**
- **On screen:** *"Everyone holds a slice. We hold the machine's whole life."*

### Act 4: The engine (slides 12–13, ~1:30)
**Slide 12 · Business model (COO, 0:50)**
- **On screen:** *"₹40, only when the fix works."*
- **Say:** "About 7% of a job. On our measured numbers alone (recovered jobs and avoided trips), an agency gets ₹4,550 of value for a ₹3,690 fee. Counting the owner's time it's about 2x. Unit economics: ₹4,216 a month per agency, 81% contribution, 6-month payback, 6.3x LTV to CAC on a conservative 3% churn."

**Slide 13 · The plan (COO, 0:40)**
- **On screen:** *"From 14 agencies to 3,800, and 34 lakh machine records."*
- **Say:** "Growth comes from field-sales capacity, and our COO's own pace backs it. Year 5: 3,793 agencies, ₹19 crore revenue, +₹2.6 crore EBITDA. Revenue per agency rises a third as plans, parts and claims attach. That's the record becoming the business. The path to ₹100 crore runs through Tier 1, plans and claims."

### Act 5: Who, trust, the ask (slides 14–16, ~2:20)
**Slide 14 · Team and backers (COO, 0:50)**
- **On screen:** *"We've built this network before, and sold it."*
- **Say:** Prathyusha built and sold FixNear; Sri Charan built Circuit; Kavyanth ran a 300+ partner network and closed most of our agencies. Full-time since May 2025. Backers: operator angels, SISFS, CIE-IIITH, the IIIT-H speech lab, our trade network. Google for Startups is unlocked by this round.

**Slide 15 · Trust (CTO, 0:30)**
- **Say:** "AI reads and drafts; rules decide money, safety and law. Zero safety incidents in nine months."

**Slide 16 · The ask (CEO, 1:00)**
- **On screen:** *"₹4.75 Cr to take Circuit from 14 agencies to 260, and reach Series A from strength."*
- **Say:** "₹4.75 crore from one lead, at ₹19 crore pre-money: your own return maths. The biggest share, 38%, proves product–market fit at scale. It buys 40,000 records by month 13, 120 agencies by month 15, and then a Series A from strength."
- **Close, and stop talking:** *"Keepwell becomes the record everyone checks before touching a machine."*

## 7. Q&A
The 16 two-sentence answers are in [`proof_pack/investor_qa.md`](proof_pack/investor_qa.md). The five most likely:
1. **"Why only 14 agencies?"** We proved price, accuracy and retention first, growing ~19% a month with no salespeople. The seed buys the sales team.
2. **"Why would an agency pay to make fewer trips?"** 13 of 18 owners say repeat visits are unpaid. Recovered jobs alone (measured) cover two-thirds of our fee.
3. **"What stops a big player copying you?"** Features, yes. Verified outcomes and a lifetime record, no.
4. **"Which line takes you past ₹100 Cr?"** Tier 1 plus plans and claims gets ~₹66 Cr by year 8. ₹100 Cr+ needs price reviews and new categories, and we say so.
5. **"Why ₹19 Cr?"** It's your own VC-method price: at ₹19 Cr the base case returns 35%.

**Never say:** "DPDP-compliant", "AI diagnosis", or a number that isn't in the model.
