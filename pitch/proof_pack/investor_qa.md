# Keepwell — Investor Q&A bank
Each answer is at most two sentences. Say it, then point to the appendix slide. Source: `../steps/12_storyboard_redteam.md` §12.4, plus the red-team additions in §12.7.

| # | Question | Answer | Slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why would an agency pay to make fewer trips if a second visit earns a second fee? | 13 of 18 owners say repeat visits for the same complaint are mostly unpaid, so they're a cost. Even with zero trips saved, recovered jobs plus owner time (~₹5,300–5,700 a month) exceed the ~₹4,900 fee at full routing. | A3, A5 |
| 2 | Five agencies for four weeks isn't traction. | It's a paid design-partner pilot. The seed's first experiment runs it at 30+ agencies and 3,000+ jobs with randomised price arms. | A4, A6 |
| 3 | What stops Urban Company or Servify copying you? | They can copy features, not verified repair outcomes or a record that follows the machine. Their models take the customer or serve the brand — the opposite of arming the agency. | 8, A13 |
| 4 | Your agency count isn't official. | No one has one. Ours is triangulated from the government's technician estimate, the repair-jobs count and the ₹ market, and all three agree. | A1 |
| 5 | Which line takes you past ₹100 Cr? | Tier 1 Circuit, plus plans and claims at record scale, gets to ~₹72 Cr by year 8. ₹100 Cr+ comes in years 9–10 with plan attach rising and the next categories. We show the conservative exit too. | A10 |
| 6 | Is ₹20 Cr pre-money justified? | Your own VC-method maths gives ₹27.4 Cr post and we ask ₹24.75 Cr. At our price the base case returns ~37% IRR (6.7x), above the 35% hurdle. | A10 |
| 7 | Telugu speech recognition is bad on phone lines. | 21% raw word error rate falls to 15% with our lexicon. We read back every note, abstain and ask 18% of the time, and the IIIT-H lab builds the golden sets. | A4 |
| 8 | What if the AI misses a gas leak? | It can't release a hazardous job. The hard-stop is a rule layer outside the model, and safety calls are human-only. | 15, A14 |
| 9 | Gemini prices double on 1 Jan 2027. | We budget at 2027 prices (₹4.5 a job), and AI cost moves LTV:CAC by only ±0.5x. The binding risk is commercial, not tokens. | A7 |
| 10 | Can you legally sell warranty plans? | We don't underwrite. We distribute insurer-filed plans on commission, gated by a legal opinion. | A14 |
| 11 | Why 24 months and ₹4.75 Cr? | The gates land around months 13–18 and a Series A takes 4–6 months. Eighteen months would force a distress raise; the extra 6 months cost founders ~6 points. Credits and SISFS are not in the ask. | A9 |
| 12 | Are the founders committed? | All three full-time since Oct 2026 (the M.Tech final project is Keepwell). 97% founder-owned, ₹15L of our own money in, salaries below market, vesting with a cliff, IP assigned. The CEO's FixNear buyer isn't a competitor and her non-compete ended in 2024. | A11 |
| 13 | What if WhatsApp changes the rules? | We already bill in INR, as required from 2026. Messaging moves unit economics by only ±0.1x, and the channel is the agency's own number. | A7 |
| 14 | Is the root-cause accuracy good enough? | 74% in beta on ~125 jobs in week 4, just under our 75% gate. About half the gain came from manuals, half from reviewed verified outcomes. E2/E4 test it at scale. | 7 |
| 15 | If an agency churns, what happens to its data? | De-identified outcomes stay in Mistri Gyan under the agency agreement's licence; personal data goes back to the agency; households keep their own record. | A14 |
