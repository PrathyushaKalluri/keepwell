# Keepwell — Voice of the customer
The household quotes are **[Real]**: they come from the case-competition interviews (7 consented interviews, logged in `ken-case-competition-2026/evidence/consent/CONSENT_LOG.md`). Speakers are shown by role only, because they are private individuals. The agency findings are **[Story]**: the 18 interviews assumed for the pitch (Step 6).

## Households: "nothing in a repair can be checked" [Real]
> "They are never going to say the price, they're going to just give you an estimation… three thousand to five thousand." — *Young professional, Hyderabad*

> "Even a duplicate part will have 3 months of warranty and will work for 3 months at least. We won't know if the part is duplicate or original." — *Homemaker, Andhra Pradesh*

> "If we visit a service station run by the company… whatever they say I will listen. If we visit a local man definitely doubts will arise." — *Retired professional*

> "I had a small door repair. When I asked him to come, he came and did it immediately. He took the amount genuinely. Since then, I have been able to trust him and approve his small works." — *Homeowner who manages two properties in other cities through one tradesman*

> "Back to back we have to call them… whether they are coming or not." — *Household interviewee*

**Survey (19 responses, 12 qualified) [Real]:**
- In **7 of 12** repairs, the person deciding, approving, paying and opening the door was not the same person.
- **7 of 12** households keep **no service record** at all.
- **47%** of 110 coded public complaints are about no-shows and chasing.

## Agency owners: 18 interviews, Oct 2026 [Story, Step 6]
- **0 of 18** use any field-service software. 16 of 18 run on phone and WhatsApp only.
- About **1 in 8 requests is lost**: calls missed while on a job, voice notes never followed up.
- **13 of 18** say a repeat visit for the same complaint is mostly unpaid.
- **14 of 18** prefer paying per completed job. The median acceptable fee is ₹30–50.

## Pilot owner feedback [Story, Step 6]
- **4 of 5** owners would be "very disappointed" if Circuit went away.
- ₹100-arm owner who halved usage: *"It takes too much of a small job."* This is why we price at ₹40.
