46°C · a fever
A retired miner, 74, home alone and running a fever. At noon, his AC stops cooling.
0 missed calls
Ramesh, the one man he trusts, is up a ladder across town. The Telugu voice note is never heard.
700 km away
His son in Bengaluru calls everyone he knows. Nobody can say what’s wrong, who will come, or when.
2 nights
He sits through two nights of that heat under a table fan before anyone opens the machine.
Nobody is the villain.
The system has no memory.
The fault, when someone finally looked: a ₹350 capacitor. By then the family had already ordered a ₹38,000 new AC, three days away.
