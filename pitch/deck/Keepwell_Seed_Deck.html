Why we exist

A ₹350 part. A ₹38,000 new AC. Between them sits a repair system with no memory.

The problem

15 crore repairs a year. ₹550 Cr of wasted trips. 27 crore machines with no memory.

And the 37,500 agencies that do the work run on a phone and a notebook.

15 Crappliance repairs a year in India
₹550 Crof technician time lost to repeat visits every year*
27 Crfridges, washing machines and ACs with no shared record

The agency

  • Calls missed on a job; Telugu voice notes never become jobs — ~1 in 8 requests lost
  • Technicians go in blind: no model, no history, wrong part
  • Nothing about the machine is ever written down

The household

  • 55% got an inherently defective machine; only 21% of those who acted were made whole
  • Price, part and "job done" can't be checked
  • Consumer helpline complaints: 13.16 → 20.49 lakh in a year, ~30% on WhatsApp

*If India matches the global 77% first-visit fix rate (no India figure exists). Sources: repo; LocalCircles; ICAP/CEEW; CLASP/BEE 2024; NCH.
