ESOP – Employee's Perspective
Understanding Employee Stock Option Plans from the employee's viewpoint - your
rights, opportunities, and what it means for your financial future.
ESOP – Employee's Perspective
  Right to buy shares later | Quantum based on Performance
  You receive the option to buy shares at a predetermined price in the future



  Vesting: 1–5 years
  Your options become available gradually over time



  Pay "exercise price," get equity
  When you're ready, you pay the agreed price to convert options into actual shares



  Sell at IPO, acquisition, buyback
  Your payday comes when the company goes public, gets acquired, or buys back shares



  Not salary, but ownership
  ESOPs represent a stake in the company's future success, not immediate compensation

ESOPs – Employee Watchlist & Pitfalls
  Leaving startup? Window to exercise
  You typically have a limited time period to exercise your vested options after leaving the company


  No liquidity = no payout
  Without an IPO, acquisition, or buyback program, your options may remain worthless on paper



  Dilution risk if startup raises rounds
  New funding rounds can reduce your percentage ownership of the compan


  Red flag: "Promise only," unclear docs
  Beware of verbal promises without proper legal documentation and clear terms
