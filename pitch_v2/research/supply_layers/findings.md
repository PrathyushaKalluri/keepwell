## F3. Technician earnings: can we recruit and keep technicians at ~₹30k a month?

**Answer.** Yes. ₹30k is above the Tier 2/3 market wage and about equal to Urban Company's average net. It only holds if the job volume behind it is real (see F4).

**Earnings evidence**

## I6 and T7. Leakage

**No Indian rate is published.**

**Urban Company's controls**
- Booking or sharing contacts off the app breaches the partner agreement and leads to deactivation.
- Damage cover is void on off-app jobs and cash payments.
- A minimum-earnings guarantee was introduced in 2021, partly to curb off-app jobs.
- Sources: [Urban help page](https://urban.co/en-gb/hero/article/What-should-I-do-if-a-client-asks-to-book-me-outside-of-Urban), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/amid-partner-protests-urban-company-ceo-backs-policies-claims-pre-tax-profitability-in-q1).

**Measured rates (all outside India)**
- **Home-cleaning app:** about 1.2% of relationships went off-platform. ([PlatStrat 2023](https://questromworld.bu.edu/platformstrategy/wp-content/uploads/sites/49/2023/06/PlatStrat2023_paper_27.pdf))
- **Freight platform:** adding a commission raised leakage about 4 percentage points, doubling it. ([PKU HSBC](https://english.phbs.pku.edu.cn/info/3882/48882.htm))
- **Gu and Zhu:** once trust in the worker is high, disintermediation offsets the platform's gain. ([Management Science 2021](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3583))
- **Blocking the off-platform channel** cut disintermediation about 18%. ([Gu, Management Science 2024](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormnsc:v:70:y:2024:i:11:p:7868-7891))

**Why Keepwell's exposure is higher than a cleaning app's**
- Repairs are infrequent, and customers keep the technician's number. The team's own evidence includes a customer taking a UC technician's number.
- 44% of AC service already goes to local technicians (LocalCircles 2026).

**Leakage of repeat jobs, LOW / BASE / HIGH:** 5% / 15% / 30%. ASSUMPTION.

**Mitigations Keepwell can build**
1. The guarantee and damage cover apply only to jobs booked and paid through Keepwell.
2. The QR sticker on the appliance points to the retailer and Keepwell.
3. Masked phone numbers.
4. Keepwell keeps the appliance history.
5. Pooled-price parts only on Keepwell jobs.
6. A guaranteed minimum income in the trough.

The structural defence: the retailer owns the customer relationship.
- *To settle:* compare each technician's repeat jobs against the installed base, and run mystery calls to customers.

**Principal vs agent** (reasoning)
- **Principal:** gross revenue, 18% GST on the whole bill, and no input credit on unregistered technicians.
- **Agent:** net revenue, with GST on the commission only. Section 9(5) may still apply.
- **The killer point:** a local mechanic below ₹20 L charges no GST. If ₹650 includes GST, the GST is ₹99, nearly the whole ₹110 installation contribution.

## What it means for the business model

1. **Productivity.** The pitch is about 1.8x UC. ₹30k at about 49 jobs a month needs about ₹610 a job, which pushes payout to about 50% of revenue.
2. **GST as principal** can erase installation contribution.
3. **Seasonality is 3x.** Pay per job plus a trough minimum, and schedule AMC visits into the trough.
4. **The layers, ranked by evidence:**
   - AMC is about 10% at ₹1,200.
   - Parts margin is UNKNOWN.
   - Refurbishment is about 15% gross and needs capital.
   - EPR is minor.
5. **Statutory, insurance and technology costs are small:** about 1–6% plus about ₹5 a job.

**Verdict:** a margin above 25% is not supported today. Services run 8–17%. Getting above 25% needs parts margin of 20% or more, AMC at ₹1,500 or more with visits at ₹300 or less, and 3+ jobs a day, all at once. All three are pilot tests.

## Killer facts

1. UC averages 43 orders a month and ₹28,322 net. The pitch has 76.
2. Voltas Apr–Jun is 35.8% of the year against 14.9% for Jul–Sep. The industry sells 60–67% of ACs in Mar–Jun.
3. UC's 2-service AC plan is ₹1,098–1,298, against the pitch's ₹1,800.
4. A Tier 2 mechanic earns ₹10–25k a month.
5. GST is 18% on services, and the ₹50 L composition limit is outgrown at one cluster. Mechanics under ₹20 L charge no GST.
6. The labour codes are in force from 21 Nov 2025, but the central levy is not notified. Karnataka charges 1%, Telangana 1–2%, Andhra Pradesh nothing.
7. The EPR floor of ₹22/kg is in court, and collectors don't get certificates.
8. Technology costs under ₹5 a job.
9. There is no mandatory refrigerant licence. R-22 servicing ends in 2030.
