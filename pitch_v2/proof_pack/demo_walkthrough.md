# Demo walkthrough: one appliance's life on Keepwell's AI desk (2 minutes, CTO)

> This is a scripted walkthrough of the WhatsApp flow, for the live demo or a screen recording. The guardrails are design rules (Step 5).

| # | Moment | What the audience sees | What the CTO says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **At the retailer counter** | The retailer adds "Keepwell install + Protect (₹2,499, AC)" to the bill. The family gets a WhatsApp consent message in Telugu and taps "Yes" | "The retailer sells. The family consents on WhatsApp. Nothing is installed on their phone." |
| 2 | **The record** | A photo of the AC's label and invoice. The model, serial and date are filled in automatically, and the plan certificate arrives | "We read the label and invoice from photos. This is the appliance's record from day one." |
| 3 | **Install** | The family picks a slot within 24 hours. The technician's card shows the authorised brand, the address and the kit | "The install is under the brand's authorisation, so the warranty is safe." |
| 4 | **Two years later** | A 12-second voice note in Telugu: "AC cooling ledu" (the AC isn't cooling) | "No forms. A voice note, in their language." |
| 5 | **Triage** | The job card shows the likely faults (gas leak at the flare joint; capacitor), the parts to carry, "**Covered by Protect until Mar 2029**", and a **safety flag: R32, flammable; brazing rules apply** | "The AI drafts the job. The plan check and the safety stop are rules, not AI guesses." |
| 6 | **Fix and verify** | The technician confirms "flare-joint leak", uploads before and after photos, and the customer gives an OTP. The record updates and the retailer gets its monthly summary | "A verified fix closes the job. That is what makes the record trustworthy to brands, and it is our Know layer." |
| 7 | **What the AI never does** | A claim-rejection attempt is routed to a person; the price comes from the rate card | "AI reads and drafts. People decide money, safety and refusals." |
