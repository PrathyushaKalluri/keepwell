## The pitch, slide by slide

### 1. Keepwell keeps the promise the retailer made

**Ravi's week.** Ravi runs a multi-brand appliance store in Guntur, a local retailer.
- On Monday he sells a ₹38,000 AC.
- By Thursday the family is back at his counter, because the brand's installer hasn't come.
- Two years later the AC breaks, and a mechanic guesses at the fault.

**Ravi earns nothing after the sale, but he carries all the blame. Keepwell is the after-sale service arm of every retailer like Ravi.**

**What the slide shows:** the title, the tagline and the three founders.

### 2. Retailers sell the appliance, then lose the customer

Three things go wrong:
- **Slow installs.** A brand install takes 2–5 days, and the family calls the retailer. 51 of the 64 retailers we met take the blame.
- **Guesswork repairs.** 80–91% of machines in homes are out of warranty. 44% of AC service goes to local mechanics, with no fixed price, no guarantee and no record.
- **Blame, no income.** The retailer nets 2–3% on the sale and earns nothing afterwards, yet consumer courts have held sellers jointly liable.

**The result:** 7 of 12 households we met keep no service record, and the person they trust has no way to keep the promise he made.

### 3. The retailer sells the promise. Keepwell keeps it.

The slide states the vision in one sentence, then shows three steps:

| Step | When | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Serve** | Live | Authorised installs in 24 hours, guaranteed repair and AMC, and **Keepwell Protect**, a 2-year repair plan. All under the retailer's name |
| **Supply** | Next | Genuine parts bought centrally; existing service centres run new clusters as partners; trade-ins collected |
| **Know and circulate** | Later | The appliance record verifies installs and claims for brands; old units are refurbished or recycled |

Each step runs on the record the step before creates.

### 4. From the retailer's bill to a second life

A flowchart shows one appliance's life and what Keepwell does at each stage:

| Stage | What Keepwell does |
|---|---|
| **Sold** | The retailer adds the install and Protect to the bill; the family consents on WhatsApp; the record starts |
| **Installed** | An authorised technician installs within 24 hours and registers the warranty |
| **In use** | AMC visits, Protect cover after the brand warranty ends, reminders |
| **Breaks** | A Telugu voice note on WhatsApp; the AI desk drafts the likely fault and the part to carry |
| **Diagnosed** | The technician confirms the fault, and asks: is it worth repairing? |
| **If yes: Parts** | Keepwell buys genuine parts centrally |
| **If yes: Repaired** | Photo and OTP close, a 90-day guarantee, and the appliance goes back into use |
| **If no: Traded in** | Keepwell collects the old unit while the retailer sells the new one |
| **If no: Refurbished or recycled** | The unit is refurbished and resold, or recycled through registered recyclers |

Every step is written to one consented record.

## Tough questions

**"Profitable from year one, so why raise?"**
Opening a cluster costs about ₹20 L before it earns, and working capital grows with revenue. Profits alone would open clusters slowly. ₹2.5 Cr opens 8 clusters in two years.

**"How do the pilot numbers fit together?"**
They are all 8-month totals for one cluster:
- Retailers × ₹19,500 a month = ₹38 L revenue.
- × 26% = ₹9.9 L contribution.
- − ₹8 L team = ₹1.9 L profit.
- The ₹5,070 a month is the per-retailer contribution that makes 20 retailers the breakeven.

**"Why ₹10 Cr pre-money?"**
- It is the course scorecard value (89/100).
- It sits inside comparables (₹6–12 Cr).
- It is about 2.9× year-1 revenue.
- It is far below the VC-method ceiling (₹47 Cr post).

**"What if it goes slower?"**
In the conservative case (10% less revenue per retailer, 25% slower rollout), year 1 breaks even and profits start in year 2. The peak need is ₹2.6 Cr, kept within the round by delaying two launches by a quarter.

**"Is Protect insurance?"**
No. It is a repairs-only service contract we deliver ourselves, on counsel-reviewed terms, with claims money ring-fenced. The formal legal opinion is due by month 6.

**"Why will retailers stay?"**
- Their name stays on every job, and they earn a commission on it.
- The after-sale blame goes away.
- 33 of the 34 pilot retailers are still with us.

**"What do the founders lack?"**
None of us has taken a startup to an exit. We close that gap with an advisor who scaled home services to 20+ cities, and with the lead investor's director on our board.
