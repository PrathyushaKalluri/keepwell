## 9.2 Valuation (course Module 4: comparables plus the VC method; the scorecard as a check)

**1. Comparables**
- Indian angel and seed rounds in 2025–26: ₹1.5–2.5 Cr raised at **₹6–12 Cr pre-money**.
- The course's CCPS template: ₹7.5 Cr pre-money.
- Our pre-seed cap: ₹6 Cr post-money (December 2025, before the pilot).
- ₹8 Cr pre-money is a **1.3× step-up** after a pilot with 34 shops, about 2,400 jobs and ₹25 L of revenue.

**2. VC method** (target IRR 35–40% over 3–5 years; Funding sheet)

| | BASE | Stress |
|---|---|---|
| Year-5 (2031) enterprise value | ₹152 Cr | ₹106 Cr |
| Share of the seed stake kept after Rounds 2 and 3 | 68.5% | 68.5% |
| Maximum seed post-money for a 35% / 40% IRR | ₹23.3 Cr / ₹19.4 Cr | ₹16.2 Cr / **₹13.5 Cr** |
| **Our post-money** | **₹10.5 Cr** | **₹10.5 Cr** |

**3. Course scorecard (check)**
- Formula: benchmark valuation × our score ÷ benchmark score.
- ₹9 Cr × 73/100 ÷ 80 ≈ **₹8.2 Cr pre-money** (Step 11).

**Conclusion: ₹8 Cr pre-money, ₹10.5 Cr post.** It is the scorecard answer, inside the comparable range and well under the strictest VC-method ceiling. The price leaves the investor room, which is part of the pitch.

## 9.5 Seed-investor return (Funding sheet; after dilution from Rounds 2 and 3)

**How the exit is valued:**
- **The formula:** enterprise value = operating revenue × 2.5 + data revenue × 7.
- **The checks:** ₹376 Cr is 2.9× year-7 revenue, about 25× the next year's EBITDA (₹15.3 Cr in year 8), and about 11× the EBITDA of the year-8 network once mature (₹33 Cr). A buyer is paying for a network still growing 40% a year.
- **The precedent:** Frontdoor trades at about 2.9× revenue and 10.8× EBITDA.

| Exit | Enterprise value | Seed stake | Value | Multiple | IRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Year 5 (2031), stress** | ₹106 Cr | 16.3% | ₹17.3 Cr | **6.9×** | **47%** |
| Year 5 (2031), BASE | ₹152 Cr | 16.3% | ₹24.9 Cr | 9.9× | 58% |
| Year 7 (2033), stress | ₹255 Cr | 16.3% | ₹41.7 Cr | 16.7× | 50% |
| Year 7 (2033), BASE | ₹376 Cr | 16.3% | ₹61.4 Cr | 24.6× | 58% |

**Downside cases:**
- **The month-9 checkpoint fails:** expansion stops with about ₹1 Cr in hand. An orderly wind-down returns about ₹0.5 Cr, under a 1x preference ahead of the founders.
- **No Round 3** (BASE turns cash-flow positive from year 5): the seed keeps 19.8%, so the return is higher.
