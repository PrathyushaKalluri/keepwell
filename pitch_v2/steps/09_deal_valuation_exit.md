> ### Read this first
> - **The deal:** CCPS, ₹2.5 Cr at **₹10 Cr pre-money / ₹12.5 Cr post. Investors take 20%.** Single close.
> - **Why ₹10 Cr:** the course scorecard gives ₹10.0 Cr (₹9 Cr benchmark × our 89/100 ÷ 80). It sits inside comparable Indian seed rounds (₹6–12 Cr pre) and far below the VC-method ceiling (₹47 Cr post). It is about **2.9× year-1 revenue** (₹3.5 Cr) for a business that is profitable from year 1.
