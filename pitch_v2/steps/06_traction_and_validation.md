## 6.3 Pilot design
- **Belt:** Vijayawada, Guntur, Mangalagiri and Tenali (Step 0c).
- **Shops:** 12 at the start, introduced through the CEO's distributor contacts, then signed on a rolling basis.
- **Partner technicians:** 8 at the start, 22 by October (9 core).
- **What we offer:**
  - authorised **AC and washing-machine installs** through the two service-centre sub-contracts (fridges need only a demo)
  - out-of-warranty service and repair on all four appliances
  - AMCs on ACs and water purifiers
- **Team:** a cluster manager and a dispatcher, hired in Jan 2026 for set-up. The CEO is based in Vijayawada.

## 6.9 Weak points, said before an investor does
- **One cluster, one season.** Repeatability is unproven until Warangal.
- **Authorisation rests mostly on one service centre** so far.
- **Shop commission is small (₹1.7k).** The pitch to shops is liability relief, not income.
- **Jobs per day only reached target in October.** We need more months of data.
- **Plan-seller volume fell short.**
