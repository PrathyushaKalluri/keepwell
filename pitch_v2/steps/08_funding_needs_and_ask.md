# Step 8: Funding needs and the ask

**Phase C (the money). Status: FINAL (19 Nov 2026), rebuilt on the simple model (Step 7c).**

> ### Read this first
> 1. **The ask: ₹2.5 Cr seed, CCPS at ₹10 Cr pre-money (₹12.5 Cr post; investors take 20%), single close.**
> 2. **We are not raising to cover losses.** EBITDA is positive from year 1. The money pays for **cluster launches and working capital**: about ₹20 L to open each cluster (set-up, parts stock, onboarding and training), plus stock and receivables as revenue grows.
> 3. **Sizing:** the plan's peak cash need is **₹2.0 Cr** (March 2030), and ₹2.5 Cr leaves a ₹0.5 Cr buffer. **This is the only round needed to reach 1,000 retailers.** From 2030, growth pays for itself.
> 4. **Course method:** the one-year need from the close is ₹0.77 Cr and the 18-month need is ₹1.2 Cr. We raise ₹2.5 Cr to fund the whole rollout to 1,000 retailers in one round, rather than raising again in 2028.

- 3 clusters live and the 4th opening
