A shop owner's week · Guntur

He sells an AC on Monday. By Thursday, the customer is angry at him.

Tuesday–WednesdayThe brand's installer "will come in 2–5 days". The family calls Ravi. Twice.
ThursdayThe family is at his counter — not the brand's. In 40 °C heat.

Ravi earns nothing after the sale — but he carries all the blame.

AI reads and drafts. Rules and people decide.

  • Reads model, serial, invoice from photos
  • Drafts the likely fault and the part to carry
  • Dispatches by zone and skill; writes the job card

Never the AI's call

  • Prices — always the published rate card
  • Gas and high-voltage safety stops — hard rules
  • Rejecting a Protect claim or a refund — a person
  • Sharing data — only with consent

The three questions we answer before you ask.

Is Protect insurance?

No. A repairs-only service contract we fulfil, on counsel-reviewed terms; claims ring-fenced. Formal opinion by month 6.

Are technicians employees?

Per-job partners. We pay the gig-worker levy (1.4%) and carry accident cover.

Do your installs void warranties?

Only authorised installs: two service-centre sub-contracts renewed for 2027, and a brand's letter of intent for direct authorisation.
