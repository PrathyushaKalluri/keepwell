The problem

Retailers sell the appliance, then lose the customer.

Slow installs

A brand install takes 2–5 days.The family calls the retailer, not the brand. 51 of 64 retailers we met take the blame.

Guesswork repairs

80–91% of machines in homes are out of warranty.44% of AC service goes to local mechanics: no fixed price, no guarantee, no record.

Blame, no income

The retailer nets 2–3% on the sale.It earns nothing after it, yet consumer courts have held sellers jointly liable.
The result: 7 of 12 households we met keep no service record, and the person they trust has no way to keep the promise he made.
Our answer

The retailer sells the promise. Keepwell keeps it.

Our vision
Keepwell looks after every appliance a local retailer sells for its whole life: installed right, repaired with genuine parts, kept covered, and refurbished or recycled when its time is up.
Step 1 · Live

Serve

Authorised installs in 24 h, guaranteed repair and AMC, and Keepwell Protect, a 2-year repair plan, under the retailer's name.
Step 2 · Next

Supply

Genuine parts bought centrally; service centres run new clusters as partners; trade-ins collected.
Step 3 · Later

Know & circulate

The record verifies installs and claims for brands; old units are refurbished or recycled.

Business model

How we make money

Live · ServePaid by households, through the retailer
  • Authorised install: ₹800 net per AC
  • Service and repair: rate card, from ₹550
  • AMC: ₹1,299 a year
  • Keepwell Protect: ₹2,000, 2-year plan
  • The retailer earns a commission on every job
Next · SupplyPaid by agencies and partners
  • Genuine parts: our jobs and local agencies
  • Partner clusters run by service centres (10% fee)
  • Trade-in pickups for exchange offers
Later · Know & circulatePaid by brands and buyers
  • Install and claim verification for brands
  • Replacement leads for retailers
  • Refurbished appliances; e-waste to recyclers
Revenue grows from ₹19,500 to ₹30,000 a month per retailer as Protect, AMC renewals and parts build.
