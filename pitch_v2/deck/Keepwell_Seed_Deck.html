The problem

He sells an AC on Monday. By Thursday the customer is angry at him.

  • Ravi runs a multi-brand appliance shop in Guntur. He sells a ₹38,000 AC on Monday.
  • The brand's installer quotes 2–5 days. On Thursday the family is back at his counter, not the brand's.
  • Two years later the AC fails. It's out of warranty; a local mechanic guesses, charges what he likes, keeps no record.
  • Ravi earns nothing after the sale — but carries the blame, and sometimes the legal notice.
“Installation delays get blamed on us.”— 51 of 64 retailers in our discovery
2–5 daysfor a brand install (Flipkart; LG quotes 24–72 h)
80–91%of the installed base is out of warranty
44%of AC service goes to local mechanics (LocalCircles, n=13,487)
7 of 12households in our survey keep no service record

Sources: brand and marketplace terms; LocalCircles; consumer-commission orders; Keepwell discovery and household survey.

Why now

The out-of-warranty wave meets WhatsApp-native India.

AC boomAC penetration about 10%, market growing ~15% a year; 1.8–2.5 M appliances sold a month leave warranty in 1–2 years.
Small towns buy local64% of after-sale value sits outside metros, mostly with local retailers the chains and platforms don't reach.
WhatsApp + voice AIFamilies already book, pay (UPI) and send voice notes on WhatsApp — in Telugu.
Rules formalise serviceGig-worker code, consumer liability for sellers, E-Waste Rules 2022: informal service is getting harder.
Someone will organise after-sale service in small towns. The retailer is the natural front door.

The product · one appliance's whole life

From the retailer's bill to a second life — what Keepwell does at every step.

1 SoldRetailer adds install + Protect to the bill; consent on WhatsApp; the record starts
2 InstalledAuthorised technician in 24 h; serial + photos; warranty registered
3 In useAMC visits off-season; Protect cover after the brand warranty; reminders
4 BreaksTelugu voice note; AI drafts the likely fault, part to carry, cover
5 DiagnosedTechnician confirms; rate-card price or covered by Protect
6 PartsKeepwell buys genuine parts centrally from distributors; stocks each cluster
7 RepairedPhoto + OTP close; 90-day guarantee; technician paid in 48 h
8 Traded inField arm values and collects; the retailer sells the replacement
9 RefurbishedSalvageable units refurbished, resold by retailers with a warranty
10 RecycledThe rest go to registered e-waste recyclers (E-Waste Rules 2022)
KNOW · every step is written to one consented appliance record → verified installs and claims for brands, leads for the retailer

Traction · pilot, Vijayawada–Guntur, Mar–Oct 2026

Eight months, 34 retailers — and the misses we learned from.

34retailers on 2-year exclusive agreements (33 active)
~2,400jobs: 775 installs, 1,330 service & repair, 280 plan-seller
₹25 Lrevenue; ₹5 L contribution
₹4.5kcontribution per retailer / month by month 4
6%Protect attach in a 12-retailer counter test

What worked

  • 74 units per retailer per month (target ≥60)
  • 87% installed within 24 h
  • 81% fixed first visit · 6.5% callbacks
  • 41% of AC units authorised · 0 safety incidents

What we missed, and fixed

  • Retailer signing lagged (2,516 vs 2,000 units only by Oct)
  • One sub-contract partner under-delivered
  • 2.6 jobs/technician-day (2.8 after re-zoning)
  • Plan-seller jobs 70 vs 100 a month

Pilot ledgers, Mar–Oct 2026. 28 retailers signed letters of intent before the pilot.
